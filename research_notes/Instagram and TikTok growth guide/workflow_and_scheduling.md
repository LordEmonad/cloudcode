# Solo creator workflow: organizing, repurposing, batching, scheduling and analytics for TikTok and Instagram (as of 2026-10-13)

Method note for the report writer: all research was done on 2026-10-13. Direct page fetching (WebFetch and curl) was blocked in this environment (DNS failure or proxy 403), so every finding below comes from search-engine result extracts of the cited pages, not from reading the full pages. Where an official doc (TikTok for Developers, Meta for Developers, Buffer help center) is cited, the wording comes from the search extract of that page. Prices change often and the third-party price roundups disagree with each other. Treat every price as "reported as of the date given" and check the vendor's pricing page before buying. Source types are labeled: **[Official]** = platform docs or help center; **[Vendor]** = a scheduler or tool company writing about itself or competitors; **[Press]** = news or trade press; **[Blog]** = practitioner or SEO guide.

## 1. Native scheduling: TikTok Studio, the Instagram app and Meta Business Suite

### Takeaway
TikTok's native scheduler is desktop or web only, needs a Creator or Business account, reaches about 10 days ahead, and is documented for videos only. Photo carousels are not documented and are reported as unsupported. Instagram's in-app scheduler reaches 75 days ahead with a cap of 25 scheduled posts per day, covering feed posts, carousels and Reels. Stories need Meta Business Suite on desktop or a third-party tool. Together the native tools cover a weekly cadence for free, but TikTok's 10-day window means a TikTok scheduling session at least weekly (or a third-party tool).

### Cited Findings
**TikTok Studio (native)**
- TikTok Studio lets you schedule posts up to 10 days in advance, on desktop only; the TikTok mobile app has no scheduling option. [Blog/Vendor, 2026] — [Hootsuite blog](https://blog.hootsuite.com/what-is-tiktok/amp/); [Metricool](https://metricool.com/schedule-tiktok-videos/)
- A conflicting 2026 guide says scheduling also works from a mobile *browser* (not the app). It agrees on Creator or Business account only and a 10-day maximum lead time. [Vendor, 2026] — [Socialync](https://www.socialync.io/blog/how-to-schedule-tiktok-posts-2026)
- Personal accounts do not see the scheduling option. A Creator or Business account is required. [Vendor, 2026] — [Socialync](https://www.socialync.io/blog/how-to-schedule-tiktok-posts-2026); [Hopper HQ](https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/how-to-schedule-tiktok-posts-desktop-mobile/)
- Guides disagree on when native scheduling arrived in TikTok Studio (2024 vs early 2025). [Vendor, 2026] — [AdaptlyPost](https://adaptlypost.com/blog/how-to-schedule-tiktok-posts); [DEV Community](https://dev.to/johnbuilds/can-you-schedule-a-post-on-tiktok-2026-3b2c)
- Third-party guides say TikTok's native Video Scheduler supports **video posts only**, and photo-carousel scheduling "is not documented by TikTok". You need a third-party tool to schedule photo carousels. [Vendor, 2026] — [Sked Social](https://skedsocial.com/blog/schedule-tiktok-photo-carousels); [Lemonpost](https://lemonpost.co/blog/schedule-tiktok-posts)
- Third-party tools say they schedule TikTok posts "weeks in advance", beyond the 10-day native window. [Vendor, 2026] — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/schedule-tiktok-videos/); [Hopper HQ](https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/how-to-schedule-tiktok-posts-desktop-mobile/)

**Instagram app and Meta Business Suite (native)**
- The Instagram app supports native scheduling of feed posts, carousels and Reels up to **75 days** ahead, with a cap of **25 scheduled posts per day**. [Press/Blog] — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/instagram-lets-users-schedule-posts-reels-in-the-app/470502/); [Diary Directory](https://www.diarydirectory.com/newsarticle/instagram-confirms-in-app-content-scheduling-up-to-75-days-in-advance/48149); [Crowbert, 2026](https://www.crowbert.com/how-to-schedule-instagram-posts)
- Eligibility conflict: most guides say a professional (Business or Creator) account is required. One 2026 guide says any public account could schedule as of March 2026. [Blog, 2026] — [SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/blog/how-to-schedule-instagram-posts/); [Planable](https://planable.io/blog/schedule-instagram-posts/); contradicted by [Crowbert](https://www.crowbert.com/how-to-schedule-instagram-posts)
- The Instagram app cannot schedule **Stories**. Meta Business Suite (desktop) is the native route for them. One source says Business Suite does not support interactive Story features (stickers and similar). [Blog, 2026] — [SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/blog/how-to-schedule-instagram-posts/); [Neal Schaffer](https://nealschaffer.com/schedule-instagram-posts/)
- Meta Business Suite schedules feed posts, Reels and carousels from desktop, reportedly with the same 75-day window. [Blog, 2026] — [Planable](https://planable.io/blog/schedule-instagram-posts/); [PostEverywhere](https://posteverywhere.ai/blog/how-to-schedule-instagram-carousels)
- Third-party tools can schedule further out than 75 days and also handle Stories and collab posts. [Blog, 2026] — [SocialBee](https://socialbee.com/blog/how-to-schedule-instagram-posts/)
- The Instagram app allows carousels of up to **20** items. The API, and therefore third-party schedulers, limits carousels to **10** items. [Vendor help center] — [Later Help](https://help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042773934)

### Inferences
- Zero-cost native stack: TikTok Studio on desktop (videos, 10 days ahead) plus Instagram in-app or Meta Business Suite (Reels, carousels, 75 days ahead). This needs at least one TikTok scheduling session a week, and TikTok photo carousels have to be posted manually from the phone.
- Because native Instagram scheduling happens inside Instagram's composer, music and other in-app features should be available when you schedule. That makes it the easiest native way to "add trending audio, then schedule" on Instagram. I did not confirm this against Meta documentation (see Gaps).
- The 25/day Instagram cap and roughly 15/day TikTok API cap (Section 3) far exceed any sensible posting rate for a new account, so they will not bind in practice.

### Gaps
- Could not fetch TikTok's or Instagram's official help-center pages on scheduling. The 10-day, 75-day and 25/day figures come from consistent third-party reports, not a fetched official page.
- No source confirmed whether TikTok Studio's desktop scheduler lets you attach TikTok library sounds (as opposed to the video's own audio), or whether it now supports photo posts.
- No source confirmed whether Meta Business Suite's Reels composer offers Instagram's licensed music library.

## 2. Third-party schedulers covering both platforms: features, 2026 prices, direct vs notification publishing, carousels, sounds and reach

### Takeaway
For one person running two accounts, the cheap tier of almost any major scheduler works: Buffer (about $5–6 per channel per month, so about $10–12/mo for TikTok plus Instagram), Publer, Metricool, Later Starter (about $19–25/mo) or Planoly Starter (about $14/mo annual). Hootsuite ($99+/user/mo, annual only) and Sprout are overkill. All of them auto-publish through the official APIs, so none can attach TikTok's or Instagram's full trending-sound library. To use a trending sound you either use the tool's "notification/inbox" mode and finish in the app, or use a tool-specific workaround: Publer and a few others offer a "let TikTok add a recommended sound" toggle for photo carousels. Vendor-run tests find no reach penalty for scheduled posts, but no independent study exists.

## 3. Automating posting through official APIs (TikTok Content Posting API, Instagram Graph API)

### Takeaway
Both platforms allow developer-built auto-posting, with real friction. TikTok's Content Posting API posts **private-only** until the app passes a separate Direct Post **audit**, caps posting at about 15 posts per creator per day, and imposes strict UX rules. Its "upload to inbox" mode avoids public-post audit requirements but needs manual finishing in the app (max 5 pending per 24h). Instagram's Graph API needs a Business or Creator account and Meta permissions. It supports Reels, images, carousels (10 items) and Stories, with a rolling 24-hour cap (official pages disagree between 50 and 100). Licensed music is limited to an Audio API on the Facebook Login path, restricted to audio authorized for third-party use. Neither API gives access to the full trending-sound library.

### Cited Findings
**TikTok Content Posting API**
- TikTok's official photo-post reference says all content posted by unaudited clients is restricted to private viewing mode. To lift this, the API client must pass an audit verifying compliance with TikTok's Terms of Service. [Official] — [TikTok for Developers: Photo Post reference](https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-posting-api-reference-photo-post)
- A third-party source says up to 5 users can post through an unaudited client in a 24-hour window. [Vendor, 2026] — [Vorplabs](https://vorplabs.com/agent-tools/tiktok-content-posting-api)
- Posting publicly ("Direct Post") requires a second review specifically for the Content Posting API audit. [Vendor tutorial, 2026] — [PostPeer](https://www.postpeer.dev/blog/tiktok-direct-posting-api-tutorial); [bundle.social](https://bundle.social/blog/tiktok-api-approval)
- TikTok's content sharing guidelines say the Direct Post ceiling "typically sits around **15 posts per creator account per day**". The cap is shared by every API client using Direct Post (all tools combined), and no API field returns the exact number. [Official] — [TikTok Content Sharing Guidelines](https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-sharing-guidelines); [Storrito Help](https://storrito.com/help-center/tiktok-post-limits/); [AdaptlyPost](https://adaptlypost.com/de/blog/tiktok-creator-info-max-video-post-duration-sec)
- Request-rate limits reported by a secondary source: 6 req/min for publish init and 30 req/min for status checks per user token. Not verified officially. Another vendor claims a 25 videos/day cap, which conflicts with TikTok's "about 15". [Blog, 2026] — [RapidDev](https://rapidevelopers.com/api-automations/how-to-automate-tiktok-video-posting-using-the-api); conflicting [Zernio](https://zernio.com/blog/tiktok-posting-api)
- Audit UX requirements reported by implementers: call `creator_info` first and build the post form from it; offer only the returned `privacy_level_options`, with no pre-selected default; interaction toggles (comment, duet, stitch) off by default and disabled if the creator has them off; always show Music Usage Confirmation and the Branded Content Policy on the Direct Post screen; branded content cannot be set to private (SELF_ONLY). [Vendor/GitHub, 2026] — [PostPeer](https://www.postpeer.dev/blog/tiktok-direct-posting-api-tutorial); [Outstand audit guide](https://www.outstand.so/docs/tiktok-audit); [GitHub PR example](https://github.com/mgt581/multi-post-dashboard/pull/151); [Postzen errors](https://www.postzen.dev/blog/tiktok-content-posting-api-errors)
- A privacy level not in the creator's options returns a 403 `privacy_level_option_mismatch` at init. `creator_info` describes the creator's account, not your audit status. [Vendor, 2026] — [Postzen](https://www.postzen.dev/blog/tiktok-content-posting-api-errors)
- Photo posts (carousels) are supported via the API, reportedly up to **35 images** per post, by **URL pull only**. A domain-verification step is mentioned. [Vendor, Aug 2026] — [bundle.social](https://bundle.social/blog/tiktok-photo-posting-api); [Blotato](https://www.blotato.com/api/tiktok)
- Buffer states TikTok's API does not provide access to the full music library, so trending sounds can't be added via API publishing. [Vendor help] — [Buffer TikTok page](https://buffer.com/TikTok)

**Instagram Graph API content publishing**
- Only Business and Creator (professional) accounts can publish via the API. Publishing requires the `instagram_business_content_publish` permission (Instagram Login) or `instagram_content_publish` (Facebook Login path). [Official] — [Meta: Content Publishing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/content-publishing)
- **Rate cap conflict on official pages:** one Meta content publishing page says **100** API-published posts per rolling 24 hours, while the carousel section and the `content_publishing_limit` reference reportedly still say **50**. Carousels count as one post. Live usage can be read via `GET /<IG_ID>/content_publishing_limit`. [Official, conflicting] — [Meta: Content Publishing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/content-publishing); [Meta: content_publishing_limit](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-graph-api/reference/ig-user/content_publishing_limit); analysis by [bundle.social (Aug 2026)](https://bundle.social/blog/instagram-api-rate-limits)
- A separate general throttle of about 200 calls/hour per account is claimed by one blog and was not confirmed in Meta docs. [Blog, 2026] — [InstantDM](https://instantdm.com/blog/instagram-api-rate-limits-explained-2026-developer-guide)
- Stories publish through the STORIES media type. Carousels are limited to **10** items via the API (versus 20 in-app). [Official/Vendor help] — [Meta: Content Publishing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/content-publishing); [Later Help](https://help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042773934)
- Meta's **Instagram Audio API** lets apps search original sounds and music and attach them to Reels at creation. It works only with **Facebook Login** (not Instagram Login), only for audio authorized for third-party use, and previewing a Reel with attached audio is not supported. [Official] — [Meta: Instagram Audio API](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/content-publishing/audio-api/)
- **Trial Reels via API:** third-party wrappers pass Meta's `trial_params.graduation_strategy` as `MANUAL` (stays a trial until graduated in-app) or `SS_PERFORMANCE` (auto-graduates on non-follower performance). Graduation itself is not exposed via the API. Reels only (single .mp4 or .mov), no carousels. One wrapper says the account needs to be public, professional, with about 1,000+ followers, and not all accounts have access. [Vendor docs, 2026] — [Ayrshare](https://www.ayrshare.com/apis/post/social-networks/instagram); [bundle.social API ref](https://info.bundle.social/api-reference/platforms/instagram); [PostEverywhere docs](https://posteverywhere.ai/docs/platforms/instagram)

### Inferences
- Shortest developer path: **Instagram Graph API is worth automating**. A Claude Code script can read the content database, push Reels and carousels from a public URL (e.g., an S3/R2 bucket) on a schedule, and poll insights. It needs a professional account linked to a Facebook Page (for the Facebook Login path and the Audio API) and a Meta app. Note the 10-item carousel cap.
- **TikTok via own API is less attractive**. Public posting needs an audit built around a full posting UI (creator_info-driven form, music-usage confirmation). Without the audit, posts are private-only. A pragmatic hybrid is a scheduler that already has TikTok Direct Post approval (Section 2), or the API's inbox/draft mode with a manual finish in the app, which is also where you'd add a trending sound.
- Use API automation for evergreen backlog posts with baked-in audio. Post natively, by hand, the few posts per week that ride a trending sound.

### Gaps
- Official TikTok and Meta pages could not be fetched in full. The exact current Instagram cap (50 vs 100) and TikTok request-per-minute limits remain unverified. Check `content_publishing_limit` live.
- Current Meta app-review requirements and turnaround time for `instagram_business_content_publish` were not researched. Typical TikTok audit turnaround time was not found.
- Not confirmed whether TikTok's Direct Post API supports the "auto-add recommended music" option for photo posts (vendors offer it, which implies an API parameter exists, but no official doc was seen).

## 4. Cross-posting: what has to change between TikTok and Instagram versions, and keeping clean masters

### Takeaway
Post from clean, watermark-free masters exported from the original project, never from a TikTok download. Instagram says it avoids recommending Reels carrying other apps' logos (Mosseri, Oct 2024). An April 30, 2026 "originality" update reportedly extended non-recommendation of mostly-reposted accounts from Reels to photos and carousels. Use 1080×1920, 9:16, MP4/H.264/AAC for both, and keep text and faces inside a shared safe area: about 150–220 px clear at top, 350–440 px at bottom and about 140 px on the right. There are no official pixel values. Rewrite captions per platform and add licensed or trending audio natively when needed.

### Cited Findings
- Mosseri (October 2024): a watermark with **your own** logo doesn't hurt reach, but Instagram tries not to recommend Reels carrying **other apps'** logos. TikTok-watermarked posts stay visible to followers but are kept out of recommendations and Explore. [Blog citing Mosseri] — [FrameOS](https://frameos.studio/blog/how-to-cross-post-without-watermarks); [SocialRails](https://socialrails.com/blog/instagram-watermarks-guide)
- Instagram's originality rule reportedly expanded on **April 30, 2026** from Reels to photos and carousels. Accounts whose output is mostly other people's content lose eligibility for recommendation to non-followers. Adding watermarks, crops, speed changes or credit tags does not make reposts "original". The primary Instagram announcement was not found. [Blog, 2026] — [Sociallyin](https://sociallyin.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-update-repost/); [SocialRails](https://socialrails.com/blog/instagram-watermarks-guide); [InstantDM](https://instantdm.com/blog/is-your-instagram-shadowbanned)
- One blog claims a threshold of 10+ reposts in 30 days triggers exclusion. Another says Instagram published no numeric thresholds and evaluates roughly a month of output. The 10-repost figure is unverified. [Blog, 2026] — [CrossGlobe Marketing](https://crossglobemarketing.com/blogs/instagram-original-content-algorithm-2026/); contradicted by [SocialRails](https://socialrails.com/blog/instagram-watermarks-guide)
- A 2026 commentary says TikTok is "aggressively down-ranking low-effort reposts, watermarked content, and anything that looks mass-produced". This is commentary, not documented policy. [Blog/newsletter, 2026] — [Mia Macnair Substack](https://miamacnair.substack.com/p/the-algorithm-changed-again-heres)
- **Specs:** both platforms are usually given as 1080×1920 at 9:16, MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio, 23–60 fps (about 30 fps suggested for fewer re-encode artifacts). TikTok desktop (Studio) uploads can reach about 10 GB, while in-app limits are much smaller (about 287 MB iPhone, 72 MB Android), and those in-app figures are not on an official page. No source gave a recommended bitrate. ## 6. File organization for a large backlog: folder structure, metadata scheme and tracking fields that Claude Code can generate and maintain

### Takeaway
No authoritative standard exists. This section is mostly design inference, informed by how schedulers model content (pillars with a set frequency, evergreen recycling) and by platform constraints (clean masters, per-platform exports, API caps). The recommended approach is an immutable `masters/` store, generated per-platform `exports/`, and a single SQLite database or CSV (`content.db` / `content.csv`) as the source of truth. Claude Code populates it with ffprobe metadata and its own tagging, and posting tools and analytics imports read from and write back to it.

### Cited Findings
- SocialBee's core model is a **category-based queue**: define content pillars, set a posting frequency per pillar, and recycle evergreen posts automatically. [Vendor/Blog, 2026] — [CostBench compare](https://costbench.com/compare/socialbee-vs-vista-social/); [Hackceleration](https://hackceleration.com/labs/compare/socialbee-vs-vista-social)
- Later's Starter plan is limited to 30 posts per profile per month, and Buffer's free plan to 10 queued posts per channel. Queue limits like these shape how much of a backlog can be loaded at once. [Blog, 2026] — [aiproductivity.ai](https://aiproductivity.ai/pricing/later/); [NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/buffer-pricing/)
- The API needs media at a public URL. TikTok photo posts are URL-pull only, so exports for API posting need hosting. [Vendor, 2026] — [bundle.social](https://bundle.social/blog/tiktok-photo-posting-api)
- Solo-creator workflow guides recommend batching similar tasks and giving each stage of the pipeline (idea, angle, batch create, edit once, publish, review) a place. [Blog, 2026] — [CreatorsJet](https://www.creatorsjet.com/blog/weekly-content-workflow-for-solo-creators); [SocialKit](https://socialk.it/en/blog/batch-content-creation-workflow)

### Inferences
**Recommended folder structure** (design recommendation, not sourced):
```
emo-content/
  inbox/                      # raw dump; Claude Code ingests from here
  masters/                    # immutable originals, renamed; never edited in place
    video/  image/  audio/
  projects/                   # editor project files (optional)
  exports/
    tiktok/                   # 1080x1920 MP4 H.264/AAC, no watermark, TikTok caption .txt
    instagram/                # same master, IG-specific crop/text if needed; carousels as numbered JPGs
    carousel/<id>/01.jpg ...  # multi-image sets (<=10 for IG API, <=35 TikTok API)
  thumbs/                     # cover frames
  rejects/                    # duplicates, low quality, off-brand, rights-unclear
  content.db (or content.csv) # source of truth
  analytics/raw/              # CSV exports from TikTok Studio / IG Insights / scheduler
```
**Naming convention** (recommendation): `EMO-<id4>_<pillar>_<format>_<dur>s_<slug>.<ext>`. For example, `EMO-0142_lore_vid_12s_sad-pepe-rain.mp4`, with per-platform exports suffixed `_tt` or `_ig` and a version tag `_v2`. The ID is stable forever and is the database key.

**Metadata and tracking fields** (recommendation). Each row is one *asset idea*, with child rows per platform post:
- Identity: `id`, `master_path`, `sha256` (for deduplication), `source` (original/remix/repost — flags originality risk on IG), `rights_ok` (bool), `created_at`.
- Technical (auto via ffprobe or exiftool): `type` (video/image/carousel), `width`, `height`, `aspect`, `duration_s`, `fps`, `has_audio`, `has_burned_captions`, `has_platform_watermark` (detected or flagged; must be false before cross-posting).
- Creative (Claude Code tags, human-verified): `pillar` (e.g., emo-lore/mascot, relatable-sad-meme, game clip, community/holder memes, behind-the-scenes dev), `format` (skit, POV text-on-screen, slideshow, reaction, gameplay), `hook_text` (first 1–2 s on-screen text), `hook_type` (question/POV/contrarian/visual), `audio_strategy` (original/baked-in/needs-trending), `cta` (none/follow/play game/link in bio), `caption_tt`, `caption_ig`, `hashtags_tt`, `hashtags_ig`, `crypto_mention` (bool, which routes to compliance review; see the crypto-rules notes), `evergreen` (bool), `priority` (1–5).
- Status pipeline: `status` ∈ {inbox, triaged, needs_edit, ready, scheduled, posted, retired}, `variant_of` (for hook A/B pairs).
- Per-platform post rows: `platform`, `post_type` (reel/trial_reel/carousel/story/tiktok_video/tiktok_photo), `scheduled_at`, `posted_at`, `post_url`, `post_id`, `tool` (native/buffer/api), `sound_used`.
- Performance (filled at 24h, 72h and 7d): `views`, `reach`, `avg_watch_s`, `watch_pct` (avg_watch/duration), `completion_rate`, `skip_rate` (IG), `retention_3s`, `likes`, `comments`, `shares_sends`, `saves`, `follows_gained`, `profile_visits`, `nonfollower_pct`, plus derived `sends_per_reach`, `likes_per_reach`, `follows_per_1k_views`, and a `verdict` (double-down/iterate/drop).

**Claude Code jobs** (recommendation): (1) ingest `inbox/`: hash and deduplicate, ffprobe, move to `masters/`, rename, insert rows; (2) auto-tag pillar, format and hook from filename, OCR or vision, then a human spot-checks; (3) render platform exports with ffmpeg (scale or pad to 1080×1920, re-encode H.264/AAC, add an optional own-brand logo, never the other platform's watermark); (4) produce a weekly "ready queue" CSV in the scheduler's bulk-upload format, or push via the IG API; (5) import analytics CSVs and compute the derived metrics and verdicts.

- No published standard or study on content-library taxonomies for creators was found. The structure above is a design recommendation.
- Bulk-upload CSV formats of specific schedulers (Buffer, Publer, Metricool) were not researched.

## 8. Analytics loop: metrics per platform, review cadence, double-down or drop rules, A/B testing (hook variants, Trial Reels)

### Takeaway
Instagram's head (Mosseri, Jan 2025) named **watch time, likes and sends** as the top ranking signals, and said to track **average watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach**. Sends matter more for reaching non-followers. Instagram's April 2026 Insights redesign added **skip rate** (the share of viewers who scroll past within the first 3 seconds), **share rate** and views-over-time. Instagram **Trial Reels** (shown only to non-followers, with a performance read at about 24h and optional auto-share based on the first 72h) are a built-in A/B tool. TikTok has no documented equivalent.

### Cited Findings
**Instagram**

[Blog, 2026] — [Storrito TikTok analytics](https://storrito.com/resources/how-tiktok-analytics-shifted-vanity-metrics-engagement-quality/); [InfluenceFlow](https://influenceflow.io/resources/how-to-analyze-tiktok-analytics-in-2026-your-ultimate-guide-to-growth-monetization/)

### Inferences
**Metrics to track per post** (in `content.db`, captured at about 24h, 72h and 7d):
- *Hook (first 2–3 s):* IG skip rate (lower is better); TikTok's retention curve value at 2–3 s.
- *Hold:* average watch time ÷ duration (`watch_pct`), completion rate, rewatches.
- *Spread:* sends/shares per reach (IG's most important non-follower signal per Mosseri), saves per reach.
- *Discovery:* % views from non-followers, or new viewers.
- *Conversion:* follows per 1,000 views, profile visits per 1,000 views, link or game clicks (UTM-tagged game links).

**Cadence:** a 10-minute glance daily (only to reply to comments, not to react to numbers); a 15-minute weekly review comparing posts within the same pillar; a monthly decision review over at least 8–12 posts per pillar.

**Decision rules** (heuristic, not sourced): rank posts within each platform by percentile (not absolute views, since early-account numbers are noisy). For the top quintile on sends/shares per reach or `watch_pct`, make 2–3 variants (same format with a new hook or new meme subject), which is the double-down. For the middle, change one variable (hook text, first frame, length). For the bottom quintile across 2+ attempts of a format, drop that format or pillar. Re-test winners from TikTok on Instagram as Trial Reels before posting them to followers.

**A/B approach:** on Instagram, use Trial Reels for hook variants: same body, different first 2 s and on-screen text, compared on views, skip rate and sends at 24–72h. On TikTok (no native A/B), post the variant days apart, change only the hook, and compare 3 s retention and watch %. Expect noise. Treat a single comparison as directional.

### Gaps
- No official TikTok or Instagram benchmark thresholds for retention, completion or skip rate were found. All numeric "good" thresholds are third-party.
- TikTok Studio's current exact metric names (October 2026) could not be verified from TikTok's help center.
- Whether IG Trial Reels' auto-share criteria or eligibility changed in 2026 is unverified.

## 9. Consistency: posting frequency vs growth for new accounts, and the risk of burnout or batch-dumping

### Takeaway
Buffer's large 2025 datasets point to **3–5 Instagram posts/week** and **2–5 TikToks/week** as the efficient zone. On TikTok, more posts raise the odds of a breakout without improving the median post: views per post +17% at 2–5/week, +29% at 6–10 and +34% at 11+. Buffer's 2025 consistency study found that posting in most weeks matters more than volume: about 450% more engagement per post for posting in 20+ of 26 weeks vs ≤4. All of this is correlational vendor data. Burnout is common: 52% of creators report it (Billion Dollar Boy, July 2025). No evidence was found that a large backlog "dump" helps. The platform caps (TikTok about 15/day, IG 25 scheduled/day) are far above sensible rates, and a steady drip from the backlog is the evidence-consistent choice.

### Inferences
- **Recommended cadence for a new emo account:** TikTok at 1 post/day (7/week, in Buffer's 6–10 bucket, which shows +29% views/post vs 1/week) if the backlog supports it, otherwise a minimum of 3–5/week. Instagram at 4–5 Reels or carousels per week plus 2–3 Trial Reel tests. Stories are optional, 2–3/week via Business Suite or a scheduler. Hold this steady for at least 8–12 weeks before judging, since Buffer's consistency data rewards weeks-posted more than volume.
- **Don't dump the backlog:** a large backlog is best used as a **12-week-plus drip**, not a burst. Burst-posting gains little per post (median TikTok views are flat with volume), risks the "mass-produced" look that 2026 commentary says TikTok demotes (unverified), and burns content you'd later need. On Instagram, it also risks a large share of non-original or repost-like items within the roughly 30-day originality window (unverified threshold).
- **Burnout guardrails:** the batch-day model, with only about 15 min/day of engagement, directly targets the top two burnout causes (creative fatigue and workload). Keep a 2–3-week scheduled buffer so missed days don't break the streak.

### Gaps
- No study specific to *brand-new* accounts (e.g., under 1,000 followers) on frequency vs follower growth was found. Buffer's data covers existing Buffer users.
- A search snippet claimed Buffer's TikTok sample covered only accounts with "8.8M–162M followers". This looks implausible given the 150,000-account sample, was not verifiable, and is excluded from findings.
- No data was found on whether posting many items in a short window (e.g., 10+ in a day) actively *hurts* reach on either platform. Claims of "spam" demotion are anecdotal.
- No independent (non-vendor) replication of Buffer's frequency or consistency findings was found.
