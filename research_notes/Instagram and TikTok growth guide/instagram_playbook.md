# Instagram Growth Playbook for a New Meme Account (as of 2026-10-13)

Source-type tags used below: **[OFFICIAL]** = Instagram/Meta/Mosseri statement (often via Social Media Today or TechCrunch coverage of it); **[DATA]** = third-party data study; **[BLOG]** = marketing blog or creator advice with no primary evidence. ## 1. How Instagram's ranking systems work in 2026 (signals, followers vs non-followers)

### Takeaway
Instagram's own stated top signals are still **watch time, likes per reach and sends (DM shares) per reach**. Likes matter slightly more for reaching followers ("connected"), and sends matter slightly more for reaching non-followers ("unconnected"). Mosseri restated this in Jan 2025 and again in May 2026. His Sept 2026 video series added four ranking principles: originality, recency, "breaking content" (auditioning small creators to non-followers) and model size. New posts are first shown to a small test group of non-followers and then widened if they perform.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL, Jan 2025]** In his ranking video series, Mosseri named watch time, likes and sends as the top three signals. He told creators to track average watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach. — [Social Media Today, "Instagram Shares Algorithm Insights To Inform Strategy"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/)
- **[OFFICIAL, Jan 2025]** Likes are "slightly more important for connected content, and sends are slightly more important for unconnected content" (connected means followers, unconnected means non-followers). — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/); quote repeated in [Kompozy](https://kompozy.io/news/instagram-mosseri-ranking-signals-guidance)
- **[OFFICIAL, May 2026]** Mosseri restated that engagement rates determine reach. Like rates matter more for followers and send rates more for non-followers, and shares are read as a sign of wider appeal. He told creators to compare connected reach (followers who viewed) against unconnected reach (non-followers who viewed). — [Social Media Today, "Instagram engagement rates provide insight into reach"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-engagement-rates-provide-insight-into-reach/821170/)
- **[OFFICIAL, 2024]** Earlier, Mosseri said sends per reach "correlate more, in my experience, with overall reach than anything else," and he later named "Views" plus "Sends per Reach" as the key creator metrics. — [Social Media Today, "Post Share Rates Are Now a Key Driver of Reach"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-post-share-rates-key-driver-reach/716540/); [Social Media Today, "Instagram Updates Metrics to Focus Creators on Views"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-updates-metrics-to-focus-creators-on-views/723645/)
- **[OFFICIAL, Feb 2025]** For watch time, the system weighs both the percentage of the video watched and the raw seconds watched, so longer videos aren't penalized for lower completion. — [Social Media Today, "Instagram Explains How Its Algorithm Weighs Time Spent Watching Longer Clips"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-longer-video-watch-time-versus-completion-rate/740916/); summarized in [blckalpaca](https://blckalpaca.at/en/knowledge-base/social-media/social-media-algorithms-distribution/instagram-algorithm-2026)
- **[OFFICIAL, Feb 26 2025]** The Creators blog post "Helping creators of all sizes break through" says the April 2024 Reels ranking changes are fully launched. A new Reel is shown to both followers and non-followers. Among non-followers it "is initially recommended and shown to a small audience of people we think will enjoy it," and the best performers then reach wider audiences. Listed signals include watch time, retention, shares, likes and comments. — [Instagram Creators blog](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/helping-creators-of-all-sizes-break-through)
- **[OFFICIAL, Sept 2026]** Mosseri posted a 4-part ranking video series, with parts reportedly dated Sept 3, 9, 18 and 24, 2026. Coverage names four principles: **originality** (original creators get more distribution than reposters, judged from account posting history), **recency** (it carries significant weight, so the feed stays roughly chronological), **breaking content** (posts from small creators are "auditioned" to non-followers and widened if above average) and **model size**. He urged creators to experiment and suggested Trial Reels for doing so. — [Social Media Today, "Instagram chief shares algorithm insights and posting tips"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-shares-algorithm-insights-and-posting-tips/831326/); part-by-part dates from [Bazzaal (blog)](https://www.bazzaal.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026-mosseri)
- **[OFFICIAL, Sept 2026]** Mosseri defended algorithmic ranking against chronological-feed proposals in Australia. He argued that without ranking, feeds get overrun by whoever posts most often, such as brands. — [Social Media Today, "Instagram Chief warns against eliminating algorithms"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-warns-against-eliminating-algorithms/830130/)
- **[OFFICIAL, Jan 2025]** Stories are ranked mostly from accounts you follow. The main signals are likelihood to tap, like or reply by DM. Stories are "less optimal for discovery," and a profile view is a minor factor. — [Social Media Today, "Instagram Shares Notes on Stories Ranking"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-stories-ranking-factors-2025/738541/)
- **[OFFICIAL, Aug 2025]** Mosseri debunked a myth: you do **not** need to engage with content in your niche to maximize reach, because what you consume isn't used as an audience-relevance signal for your posts. — [Social Media Today, "Instagram Chief Dispels Engagement Myth, Says Using Edits Boosts Reach"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-mosseri-says-edits-reach-boost-engagement-myths/757268/)
- **[OFFICIAL, Dec 2025 to Jun 2026]** "Your Algorithm" user controls launched for Reels in Dec 2025 and expanded to Feed and Explore in tests by June 2026. Users can add or remove topics to steer their recommendations. — [TechCrunch, Jun 27 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/27/instagram-is-testing-more-ways-for-users-to-customize-your-algorithm/); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-tests-more-algorithm-controls/823936/)
- **[OFFICIAL, Sept 2025]** Instagram reached 3 billion monthly active users. Mosseri said Reels and DMs "have driven most all our growth," and Instagram tested a Reels-first layout (DMs and Reels as the first tabs) in India and South Korea. — [TechCrunch, Sep 24 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/24/instagram-now-has-3-billion-monthly-active-users-will-test-features-to-help-users-control-their-feeds/); [TechCrunch, Sep 29 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/29/instagram-is-testing-a-reels-first-ui-in-india-and-south-korea/)
- **[BLOG, unverified]** The claim that "sends count 3–5x more than likes" appears only in marketing blogs and could not be traced to Instagram. — [Socialync (blog)](https://www.socialync.io/blog/adam-mosseri-shares-instagram-algorithm-2026); flagged as unverified by search synthesis
- **[OFFICIAL, background]** Instagram's evergreen "Instagram Ranking Explained" page is reportedly still dated May 2023, so it predates the per-reach framing. — [Instagram](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained); staleness noted in [Kompozy](https://kompozy.io/news/instagram-mosseri-ranking-signals-guidance)

### Inferences
- For a brand-new account, almost all early reach is unconnected (non-follower) reach. **Sends per reach is the metric to optimize.** Memes that people DM to a friend ("this is so you") match the top non-follower signal more directly than any other format.
- Because posts are auditioned to a small non-follower group first, follower count isn't a gate for a new account. Each post gets a test, and its per-reach rates decide whether it gets more distribution.
- Recency weighting plus auditioning favors posting regularly over occasional "perfect" posts.

### Gaps
- No official numeric weights for the signals exist. Instagram has never published the size of the like vs send difference.
- Mosseri's Sept 2026 videos and captions could not be opened directly. The "model size" principle is reported only in secondary coverage, and its meaning for creators is unclear.

## 2. Reels: length, hooks, text, captions, audio, loops, and retention/skip-rate benchmarks

### Takeaway
Reels can be **3 seconds to 3 minutes** for recommendation purposes. Longer uploads (recording up to 20 min) are allowed but are **not recommended to non-followers**. Instagram's Sept 2026 guide calls 15–60s the sweet spot for most creative formats, and Socialinsider's 2026 data shows 45–60s with the best engagement and median views. The first ~3 seconds decide the **skip rate**, which is now a core Insights metric. Typical skip rates for small accounts run around 65%. There is no rigorous evidence that trending audio beats original audio.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL, Jan 2025]** Max Reel length went from 90 seconds to **3 minutes**. Reels up to 3 min are eligible for non-follower surfaces (Reels tab, Explore). — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-officially-expands-reels-length-3-minutes/737766/); [Social Media Today, "Instagram Says It Will Recommend Longer Reels in Explore"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-will-recommend-longer-3-minute-reels/737913/); [Instagram Creators blog](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/the-latest-with-instagram)
- **[OFFICIAL, current]** Instagram's Reels feature page says you can record up to 20 minutes, but "Reels over 3 minutes won't be recommended to new audiences." — [Instagram, Reels feature page](https://about.instagram.com/features/reels)
- **[OFFICIAL, Sept 28 2026]** Instagram's updated Reels creation guide says Reels can be "anywhere from 3 seconds to 3 minutes," and calls "15 to 60 seconds: The sweet spot for tutorials, day-in-my-life clips and most creative formats." Content should be high-quality, original and engaging from the first second, and text overlays can serve as the hook. — [Social Media Today, "Instagram shares Reels creation tips in new guide"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-reels-creation-tips-in-new-guide/831551/)
- **[OFFICIAL, Sept 2026]** On text, the same guide says overlays emphasize key moments or open with a hook, while captions (subtitles) make all spoken audio readable. Captions should be included "even if the creator has already added text overlays," including for accessibility. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-reels-creation-tips-in-new-guide/831551/)
- **[OFFICIAL, ~Aug 2026]** Instagram's five-step video workflow, built from creator feedback, says to build the hook first and spend much of your editing time on the opening. It says: "If viewers are dropping off at the 4-second mark, the hook needs work." It recommends cutting on the beat with Edits' beat markers, and treating each video's Insights as input for the next edit. — [Social Media Today, "Instagram shares tips on video creation"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-tips-on-video-creation/827172/)
- **[OFFICIAL, Jan 2025]** Mosseri said Reels that are watermarked, low-resolution, muted, bordered or mostly text get less reach. — [Social Media Today, "Instagram Chief Answers Key Creator Questions…2025"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-answers-creator-questions/744813/)
- **[OFFICIAL, ~2025]** The Creators FAQ says to use a compelling hook in the first 3 seconds, upload in high resolution and avoid third-party watermarks. — [Instagram Creators FAQ](https://creators.instagram.com/faq?locale=en_US)
- **[OFFICIAL, 2025]** **Skip rate** is the percentage of views where people skip during the first 3 seconds, replacing the older "view rate." Retention graphs always slope down, and "the flatter the line, the more engaged your audience." — [Social Media Today, "Instagram Adds Retention Metrics for Reels"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-adds-retention-insights-reels/758464/); [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-reel-analytics/)
- **[DATA, 2026]** Socialinsider's skip-rate benchmarks average **~65.5% for 1K–5K follower accounts** and **~60.5% for 100K–1M** accounts, with lower skip rates for larger accounts. — [Socialinsider, 2026 Instagram Benchmarks](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram)
- **[DATA, Jan–Jun 2026, 6M Reels]** The 45–60s bracket had the highest engagement rate (0.35%) and highest median views (10,374). Engagement was fairly flat from 30s to 3 min and fell to 0.15% beyond 3 min, and under-30s Reels underperformed. — [Socialinsider, Ideal Reels Length](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-length/)
- **[DATA, conflicting]** A Socialinsider reach study of 140K business Reels found 30–60s Reels had the highest reach rate (5.60%). Another Socialinsider post says 60–90s is best for engagement, and its benchmark report singles out 45–60s and 120–180s. Emplifi found longer Reels (30–90s, median 7,830 views) beat under-30s (6,145). — [Socialinsider Reels statistics](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-statistics/); [Socialinsider Reels strategy](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-strategy/); [Emplifi](https://emplifi.io/press/emplifi-reports-instagram-reels-outperformed-other-video-content-instagram-facebook-tiktok/)
- **[DATA, weak]** No large-scale study compares trending audio with original audio. Buffer's single-creator test of three otherwise identical videos found the trending-audio version got ~24% more views than voiceover and ~33% more than ASMR. Hootsuite's 2021 six-Reel test is anecdotal and outdated. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/); [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/instagram-reels-experiment/)
- **[OFFICIAL, Aug 2025]** Using the Edits app "does help a little bit with reach. It's not going to be something that's going to be around forever." Instagram added a prediction of whether a viewer will tap the Edits button into ranking, and warned against using Edits as a pass-through ("we can check for that"). — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-mosseri-says-edits-reach-boost-engagement-myths/757268/)
- **[OFFICIAL, 2026]** Reels AI translations expanded to more languages. Creators can review the translations or turn off lip-sync before posting. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-reels-adds-more-ai-translations/825258/)
- **[OUTDATED]** Guidance that Reels must be under 90s to be recommendable, with a 30–90s "sweet spot," came from Instagram in 2024 and was superseded in Jan 2025. — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/instagram-courting-smaller-creators-us-tiktok-ban-looms-it-s-delicate-balance); [Social Media Today, "Single Word CTAs and Longer Reels"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-clarifies-advice-single-word-ctas-longer-reels/718151/)

### Inferences
- For meme video: keep most Reels 7–30s for punchy loops, and use 45–60s for skits or story-style pieces. Never go over 3:00 if you want non-follower reach. Short memes that loop cleanly push up watch time per viewer, though no source quantifies a "loop bonus" (see Gaps).
- Put a visual or text hook in frame 1 (overlay text such as the meme setup) and burn in captions. Many viewers watch muted.
- Use Edits for at least some edits while the small reach boost lasts (stated Aug 2025, temporary).

### Gaps
- No official or robust third-party data quantifies a "loop" or rewatch bonus. Loops are assumed to help through watch time, but that is inference.
- No official skip-rate target exists. The only numbers are Socialinsider averages.
- No strong evidence exists on trending vs original audio. An A/B test on the account itself is the only reliable answer.

## 3. Trial Reels

### Takeaway
Trial Reels go **only to non-followers first**. After ~24h you get metrics, and you can set them to auto-share to followers if they perform well within **72 hours**. Instagram reports they lifted non-follower Reels reach by **80%**. They are ideal for testing several hooks or edits of the same meme from a backlog. Eligibility is reported inconsistently: a professional account is required, and a public account with 1,000+ followers was reported in 2025. Up to 20 trials per day, and trials can be scheduled (since ~Mar 2026).

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL, launched Dec 10 2024]** Trial Reels are shown to non-followers first. They don't appear on your grid or in followers' feeds, though a follower could still see one if it is DM'd to them. — [TechCrunch, Dec 10 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/12/10/instagram-rolls-out-trial-reels-that-arent-shown-to-a-creators-followers/); [Instagram Creators blog](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels)
- **[OFFICIAL]** You can choose to have a trial auto-shared to followers if it performs well, "judged by the views it receives within the first 72 hours." Metrics (views, likes, comments, shares), plus comparisons with your previous trials, appear about 24h after posting. — [Instagram Creators blog](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-launches-trial-reels-audience-sampling/735136/)
- **[OFFICIAL, 2025]** Instagram says 40% of creators who used Trial Reels posted more often, and that Trial Reels led to an **80% increase in Reels reach among non-followers**. These are Instagram's own figures. — [Social Media Today, "Trial Reels Have Helped Creators Significantly Increase Reach"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-trial-reels-increase-reach-tests/750121/)
- **[OFFICIAL, eligibility conflicting]** At launch, Trial Reels needed a professional account but were "not gated by number of followers." A 2025 report says Mosseri confirmed availability to all creators with **1,000+ followers and a Public account**, and Instagram's Reels page still says a professional account is required. — [TechCrunch 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/12/10/instagram-rolls-out-trial-reels-that-arent-shown-to-a-creators-followers/); [Social Media Today, "Trial Reels Are Now Available to More Creators"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-expands-access-trial-reels/752895/); [Instagram Creators, Reels](https://creators.instagram.com/reels)
- **[BLOG]** One blog citing Meta's Help Center says the threshold is 200 followers for professional accounts and 1,000 for personal ones. This could not be verified. — [Fliki (blog)](https://fliki.ai/blog/trial-reels-instagram)
- **[OFFICIAL/REPORTED]** Limit of 20 trial Reels per day. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-expands-access-trial-reels/752895/)
- **[OFFICIAL, ~Mar 2026]** Creators can schedule when Trial Reels are shown to non-followers. — [Social Media Today, "Instagram allows creators to schedule Trial Reels"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-allows-creators-to-schedule-trial-reels/816549/)
- **[OFFICIAL, Sept 2026]** Mosseri recommended Trial Reels as the way to experiment. Coverage notes that some creators use trials to re-share older Reels to new audiences. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-shares-algorithm-insights-and-posting-tips/831326/); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-answers-creator-questions/744813/)

### Inferences
- For a backlog of existing videos, Trial Reels let you run the same meme with 2–3 different hooks or first frames and promote only the winner. This also keeps the follower grid clean while you learn what this audience responds to.
- If the 1,000-follower gate applies, a brand-new account may not see the toggle on day one. Plan to grow to that threshold with regular Reels and carousels first.

### Gaps
- The exact current eligibility threshold and the auto-share performance threshold are unconfirmed. The Instagram Help Center page could not be retrieved, so the Trial Reels toggle on the account itself is the definitive check.

## 4. Carousels and meme image posts vs Reels

### Takeaway
2026 data consistently shows **Reels win on reach to new people** (Buffer: ~2.25x single images), while **carousels win on engagement per reach and often on views and saves**. Socialinsider finds carousels slightly ahead on reach rate overall, and ahead of Reels on reach for accounts over 50K followers. Carousels get a built-in "second chance": a viewer who doesn't swipe can be re-served slide 2. Carousels with music can appear in the Reels tab. Up to 20 slides are allowed.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL, ~late 2024]** Mosseri: "multiple pieces of media are going to mean more interactions with your carousel posts, and more interactions is going to mean more reach on average… if someone sees your carousel post but they don't swipe, we'll often give that carousel a second chance and automatically move to that second piece of media." — [Social Media Today, "IG Chief Recommends Posting Carousels To Improve Reach"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/ig-chief-recommends-posting-carousels-improve-reach/730232/)
- **[OFFICIAL, ~2024–2025]** Carousels with music are eligible to appear in the Reels tab. Mosseri also cautioned against forcing carousels when the format doesn't fit. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/ig-chief-recommends-posting-carousels-improve-reach/730232/); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-answers-creator-questions/744813/)
- **[OFFICIAL, ~Aug 2024]** Carousels expanded to **20 frames**. — [Social Media Today, "Instagram Expands Carousels to 20 Frames"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-expands-carousels-to-20-frames/723792/)
- **[OFFICIAL, 2025–2026]** Recent carousel changes include rearranging slides after publishing (rolled out), per-frame like counts (in test) and per-slide captions (in test). — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-allows-users-to-rearrange-carousel-posts/815490/); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-tests-like-counts-for-frames-in-a-carousel/753516/); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-tests-captions-on-each-post-within-carousels/819917/)
- **[OFFICIAL, early Oct 2026]** The Edits app gained carousel editing tools (photo and video layouts, fonts, overlays, stickers), on iOS first with Android "soon." — [Social Media Today, "Edits app gets carousel editing tools"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/edits-app-gets-carousel-editing-tools/832448/)
- **[DATA, Mar 2026, Buffer 45M+ posts]** On Instagram, Reels reach ~**2.25x** more people than single images. Median engagement as a share of reach: carousels **6.90%**, single images **4.44%**, Reels **3.31%**. Reels' per-reach engagement looks lower because they reach so many people. — [Buffer, Best Content Format 2026](https://buffer.com/resources/data-best-content-format-social-media/); [Buffer, State of Engagement 2026](https://buffer.com/resources/state-of-social-media-engagement-2026/)
- **[DATA, Jan 2025–Aug 2026, Socialinsider 872K posts]** Average reach rate: carousels 4.50%, Reels 4.10%, images 4.00%. Engagement rate: carousels 0.55%, Reels 0.52%. Carousels lead on views at every account size. — [Socialinsider, Social Media Reach Statistics 2026](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/social-media-reach/); [Socialinsider 2026 Benchmarks](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram)
- **[DATA, Jan–Jun 2026]** In Socialinsider's 140K business-Reels study, Reels lead on reach only for accounts **under 50K followers**, and carousels lead above that. — [Socialinsider, Reels statistics](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-statistics/)
- **[DATA, 2026, Metricool]** Carousels get more than 2x the reach and views of single images and 9x more saves. Reels generate 4x+ the interactions of single images. Carousels are posted 37% less often than single images. — [Metricool 2026 Instagram stats](https://metricool.com/important-instagram-statistics/)
- **[DATA, 2026]** Socialinsider (70M posts) puts the average Instagram engagement rate at 0.48%, flat through 2025, with comments per post down 16%. — via [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-engagement-rates-provide-insight-into-reach/821170/)
- **[OFFICIAL/specs, Jan 2025]** The profile grid moved to a 3:4 "tall grid," and Instagram natively supports 3:4 images (1080x1440). Feed images can range from 3:4 to 1.91:1, and the first slide sets the aspect ratio for the whole carousel. — [Social Media Today, "Instagram Adds Native Support for 3:4 Images"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-adds-support-phone-camera-aspect-ratio-images/749205/); [Buffer image size guide](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-image-size/)
- **[DATA, note]** Buffer says carousels published via its tool are capped at 10 images by the API, versus 20 in-app. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-image-size/)

### Inferences
- For a meme brand with a big image backlog, **multi-meme carousels with music** (for example "5 emo moods," or a panel-by-panel comic of Emogotchi) give three chances at distribution: the Feed, the second-chance slide-2 reshow, and the Reels tab via music. Single static memes are the weakest format by all datasets.
- At under 50K followers, Reels are the main reach engine (Socialinsider). Mix Reels, which find new people, with carousels, which drive engagement, saves and shares among people who already see you.
- Make slide 1 a strong standalone meme and slide 2 a second hook, because slide 2 is what non-swipers get re-served.
- Export images at 1080x1440 (3:4) so they fill the tall grid and feed.

### Gaps
- No dataset isolates *meme pages* specifically. All the format studies above are business or creator samples.
- No data quantifies how much the Reels-tab placement of music carousels adds.

## 5. Originality rules, aggregator penalties and watermarks

### Takeaway
As of **April 30 2026**, an account whose posts in a rolling 30-day window are **mostly reposts** of others' work is classed as an aggregator. It becomes **non-recommendable across Reels, photos and carousels**: followers still see it, but non-followers do not. Watermarks, speed changes, light crops and credited screenshots do **not** make content original. Memes built on templates **are** original if you add humor or commentary. Reels carrying another app's watermark (TikTok) have been denied recommendation since 2021, while your own logo is fine.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL, Apr 30 2026]** The Reels aggregator protections now also apply to photos and carousels. Accounts that mostly repost others' work won't be recommended anywhere, including Feed recommendations and Explore/Discover, but followers still see their posts. — [TechCrunch, Apr 30 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/); [Social Media Today, "Instagram updates algorithm to benefit original creators"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-updates-algorithm-to-benefit-original-creators/819016/)
- **[OFFICIAL, Apr 2026]** "Instagram will assess accounts on a rolling month-by-month basis. If most of what an account posts within that 30-day period are re-posts, it will be considered an aggregator account." Publishers with licensing agreements or explicit creator permission are exempt. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-updates-algorithm-to-benefit-original-creators/819016/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/)
- **[OFFICIAL, Apr 2026]** Materially edited content counts as original. Low-effort edits such as watermarks or speed changes do not, and neither does uploading a screenshot of someone else's post with their username visible as credit. To share others' work, Instagram suggests Collabs, Remix or the paid-partnership label. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/)
- **[OFFICIAL, per search synthesis of TechCrunch/Instagram blog]** Using third-party material such as meme templates or popular clips is fine if you add an element that enhances it. A meme creator qualifies by adding humor, social commentary, cultural references or a relatable take. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/)
- **[OFFICIAL, Apr 30 2024]** When Instagram finds two or more identical pieces of content, it recommends only the original. It won't replace content that has been significantly changed, "such as memes or parodies." Reposts get labels linking back to the original creator. — [TechCrunch, Apr 30 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/04/30/instagram-is-updating-its-ranking-systems-to-surface-more-content-from-smaller-original-creators/)
- **[OFFICIAL, 2024]** An account flagged as an aggregator regains recommendation eligibility 30 days after its last unoriginal post. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-algorithm-prioritize-original-emerging-creators/714777/)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Instagram's Recommendation Guidelines list as low-quality: "unoriginal content that is largely repurposed from another source with only minor, immaterial edits," long captions unrelated to the content, and coordinated comment networks. "About original content" says that stitching clips together or adding a watermark is not material editing. — [Instagram Help, Recommendations on Instagram](https://help.instagram.com/313829416281232/); [Instagram Help, About original content](https://help.instagram.com/1800814370401535/)
- **[OFFICIAL, ~2024]** Mosseri said watermarking a Reel with **your own logo** does not hurt reach, but Instagram "tries not to recommend Reels with logos from other apps." — [Social Media Today, "Including Your Own Logo on a Reel Is Fine"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-clarifies-including-your-own-logo-on-a-reel-is-ok/730852/)
- **[OFFICIAL, 2021, still referenced]** Reels showing a TikTok watermark are limited in recommendations. The Creators FAQ still says a "3rd party watermark on your reel may be impacting its reach." — [Social Media Today, 2021](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-will-now-limit-the-reach-of-re-posts-from-tiktok-within-its-reels/594803/); [Instagram Creators FAQ](https://creators.instagram.com/faq?locale=en_US)
- **[UNVERIFIED]** A figure of "10 or more unoriginal posts in 30 days" circulated in 2024 (Nasdaq) and is repeated by blogs, but it was not confirmed in Instagram material found. The 2026 wording is a "mostly reposts" ratio. Blog figures of "40–60% more distribution for originals" and "60–80% reach drops for aggregators" are also unverified. — [Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/instagram-updates-its-reels-algorithm-to-prioritize-original-content); [Sociallyin (blog)](https://sociallyin.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-update-repost/)

### Inferences
- The emo brand posting **its own memes, its own art and its own Emogotchi footage** is original by Instagram's definitions. Even memes on popular templates qualify when the joke or caption is theirs.
- The risk is **mixing in reposted or screenshotted community memes**. If those become a majority within any 30-day window, every post loses non-follower reach. Share community content through Collab invites, Remix, or Story reshares (Stories aren't a discovery surface anyway) rather than as feed reposts.
- Any backlog video previously posted on TikTok must be re-exported from the original project file without the TikTok watermark or end card. The emo logo can stay on.
- If the same video already exists on Instagram from another account (fans reposting), Instagram's dedupe should favor the earliest original. Whether that is the brand's upload is not guaranteed if fans posted first (see Gaps).

### Gaps
- No primary source spells out how Instagram decides which copy is "the original" when the creator uploads after a fan repost.
- The exact "majority" share and how the 30-day window is computed are not public.

## 6. Posting cadence, timing, Stories and Broadcast Channels

### Takeaway
More posting correlates with faster growth: 3–5 feed posts per week roughly doubles follower growth versus 1–2, and 6–9 per week correlates with ~3.7x growth, with diminishing returns. Instagram says its fastest-growing creators post **10+ Reels a month**. Mosseri says posting time has **no direct correlation** with reach, and "best time" studies disagree. Stories serve existing followers rather than discovery, and resharing your own post to Stories doesn't raise its reach. Broadcast Channels require a public account with 1,000+ followers.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL]** "Creators with the greatest net follower growth rates post, on average, 10 or more reels per month," though frequency is "just one factor of many." — [Instagram Creators FAQ](https://creators.instagram.com/faq?locale=en_US); [Social Media Today, ~Feb 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-reels-recommendations-tips/741030/)
- **[DATA, 2026, Buffer 52M+ posts]** Posting 6–9 times per week correlates with ~3.7x the follower-growth increase of 1–2 per week. Reach per post is ~12% higher at 3–5 per week, ~18% at 6–9 and ~24% at 10+ versus 1–2, with diminishing returns. — [Buffer, How Often to Post on Instagram](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/); [Buffer, State of Engagement 2026](https://buffer.com/resources/state-of-social-media-engagement-2026/)
- **[DATA, 2025, Buffer 2.1M posts / 102K accounts]** Posting 3–5 times a week "can more than double follower growth rate" versus 1–2 times. This covers feed posts only, not Stories. — [Social Media Today, "New Study Suggests That Posting More Often Is Key to IG Growth"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/study-shows-posting-more-instagram-leads-to-more-reach/757633/)
- **[DATA, 2026]** Accounts that posted in 20+ of 26 weeks saw ~450% more engagement per post than those posting in ≤4 weeks. — [Buffer, Creator Growth Playbook](https://buffer.com/resources/creator-growth-playbook/)
- **[OFFICIAL, 2025]** Mosseri said there is "no direct correlation" between posting at a particular time and reach, though being around to engage after posting can bring profile visits. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-answers-creator-questions/744813/)
- **[DATA, Sept 2026, Buffer 9.6M posts]** The top slots are Thu 9am, Wed 12pm and Wed 6pm, with Wednesday the strongest day. Other studies disagree: Later (6M posts, 2024 data) picks 5am local time, and Metricool (2026) picks 8pm with Wednesday leading. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/when-is-the-best-time-to-post-on-instagram/); [Later](https://later.com/blog/best-time-to-post-on-instagram/); [Metricool](https://metricool.com/best-time-to-post-social-networks/)
- **[OFFICIAL, Apr 2026]** Mosseri said resharing a feed post to Stories won't increase the post's reach. Feed posts get expanded recommendation (Explore), while Stories show only to followers. Hack tactics "sometimes work, usually don't, and when we find them, we usually shut them down." Instagram is weighing native Stories scheduling. — [Social Media Today, "Instagram chief debunks popular engagement hack"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-debunks-popular-engagement-hack/816781/)
- **[OFFICIAL, Jan 2025]** Stories are ranked from accounts you follow, and the signals are taps, likes and DM replies. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-stories-ranking-factors-2025/738541/)
- **[OUTDATED, ~2021]** Mosseri's old "two feed posts per week, two stories per day" advice predates Reels-first ranking. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-adam-mosseri-offers-insights-on-how-to-grow-reach-algorith/601667/)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Broadcast Channels can be started by a public account with more than 1,000 followers. Rollout is gradual, and creators choose whether the channel appears on their profile. Later features added member live streams, QR codes and emoji controls. Some coverage cites a 10K threshold for certain features. — [Instagram Help, Create a channel](https://help.instagram.com/783859809732797); [Instagram announcement](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/introducing-broadcast-channels); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-features-broadcast-channels/718031/)

### Inferences
- A reasonable new-account cadence is about **1 Reel per day, or at least 10–15 Reels a month, plus 2–4 meme carousels a week**. Stories go out daily to existing followers (polls, behind-the-scenes, Emogotchi updates) to drive DM replies. With a large backlog, volume is not the constraint. Quality per post and avoiding near-duplicate posts are.
- Choose posting times from the account's own Insights once it has data. Before that, any weekday daytime slot is fine, and being present to reply in the first hour matters more than the exact minute.
- Plan a Broadcast Channel (e.g., "emo hq") once the account passes 1,000 followers, for drops, $EMO and game updates and polls.

### Gaps
- No official guidance gives a Stories frequency. No data shows Broadcast Channel effects on account growth.
- No evidence was found on whether very high volume (multiple Reels a day) cannibalizes reach per post on Instagram specifically. Buffer shows diminishing but still positive returns.

## 7. Discovery: hashtags, keywords/SEO, alt text, Instagram search, Google indexing

### Takeaway
Since **December 2025**, Instagram caps hashtags at **5 per post or Reel**, down from 30. Mosseri says hashtags don't materially increase reach but help categorize posts. Keywords in username, name, bio and captions drive Instagram search. Public posts from **professional, 18+ accounts** can be indexed by Google (expanded from July 10 2025), and Google Search Console added social-profile reporting in 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL, ~Dec 2025]** "Instagram will gradually update the number of hashtags that you can include in a caption for a reel or post to five. We find that using fewer (up to 5) more targeted hashtags, rather than many generic ones, can improve both your content's performance and people's experience." This followed a roughly year-long test that capped many users at 3. — [Social Media Today, "Instagram Implements New Limits on Hashtag Use"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/)
- **[BLOG, conflicting]** Enforcement past 5 tags is reported inconsistently: some accounts are blocked from publishing, others have extra tags ignored. It's unclear whether first-comment hashtags count toward the 5. Meta's developer (API) documentation reportedly still says 30. — [Later](https://later.com/blog/ultimate-guide-to-using-instagram-hashtags/); [Presly (blog)](https://presly.ai/blog/instagram-hashtag-limit); [Storycut (blog)](https://www.storycut.com/blog/instagram-hashtag-limit)
- **[OFFICIAL, 2025]** Mosseri said hashtags don't improve visibility but are "a great way to let people know what a post is about and connect posts." — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/)
- **[OFFICIAL, older Mosseri guidance]** "The text you enter in the search bar is by far the most important signal for Search. We try to match what you type with relevant usernames, bios, captions, hashtags, and places." Put keywords and hashtags in the caption, not the comments. — via [Later, Instagram SEO](https://later.com/blog/instagram-seo/); [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/social-media-updates/instagram/instagram-search-seo/)
- **[DATA/BLOG]** A Hootsuite experiment and 2026 guides found keyword-rich captions outperform hashtag-heavy captions for discovery. - **[REPORTED, Jul 2026]** Google Search Console added "social channel" properties covering Instagram, TikTok, X and YouTube. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-search-console-adds-social-video-platform-properties/581634/); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-opens-search-console-social-reporting-to-everyone/584144/)

### Inferences
- Use 3–5 specific tags (e.g., #emo, #emomemes, #memecoin, #monad, plus one per-post topic tag) and write captions with plain-language keywords people search, such as "emo meme," "sad cat meme" or "virtual pet game."
- Put "emo" and a descriptor in the display name field (e.g., "emo | emo memes & Emogotchi"), because name and username are searchable.
- A professional account is needed for Google indexing, which is another reason to switch.

### Gaps
- No primary source says whether first-comment hashtags count toward the cap.
- No data shows how much traffic Instagram posts actually receive from Google search.

## 10. Growth tactics: Collabs, comments, DMs, reposts, cross-posting from TikTok, Insights

### Takeaway
**Collab posts** show the same post in both accounts' feeds and grids, with shared counts, and they are Instagram's endorsed alternative to reposting. Reply to comments in the first days. Open-ended CTAs are fine, but "comment WORD" style asks can block recommendation. Use Insights (views, skip rate, retention, sends, connected vs unconnected reach) to iterate. Cross-post TikTok content only **without the watermark**.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL]** In a Collab, both accounts appear in the header, the post appears on both grids and is shared to both sets of followers, and views, likes and comments are combined. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-add-three-collaborators-posts-reels/685137/); [TechCrunch, Oct 2021](https://techcrunch.com/2021/10/19/instagram-is-adding-collabs-new-music-features-for-reels-desktop-posting-and-more/)
- **[OFFICIAL, conflicting count]** The 2023 limit was up to 3 collaborators. Later Social Media Today coverage cites a higher figure (described as 6). The current cap is unconfirmed. — [Social Media Today, 2023](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-add-three-collaborators-posts-reels/685137/); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktoks-testing-collaboration-feature-posts/715994/)
- **[OFFICIAL, Apr 2026]** Instagram explicitly recommends Collabs, Remix and paid-partnership labels as the way to feature others' work without aggregator penalties. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/)
- **[OFFICIAL, ~Feb 2025]** Like or authentically reply to comments on your Reel within the first 7 days. Make Reels that make sense to people who don't know you. Give explicit guidance in the caption on what viewers should do next. — [Social Media Today, "Instagram Provides Tips on How To Maximize Content Recommendations"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-reels-recommendations-tips/741030/)

- **[OFFICIAL]** Instagram does not reduce the reach of posts labeled as sponsored or paid partnership. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-says-does-not-reduce-the-reach-of-sponsored-content/732615/)
- **[OFFICIAL, 2021–2024]** TikTok-watermarked Reels are limited in recommendations, while your own logo is fine (see Q5). — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-will-now-limit-the-reach-of-re-posts-from-tiktok-within-its-reels/594803/); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-clarifies-including-your-own-logo-on-a-reel-is-ok/730852/)

A blog review says an April 2026 Insights redesign added share rate. ### Inferences
- **Collab with other emo, meme, Monad and crypto-meme accounts and with Emogotchi-related creators.** Both audiences see the post, and it is the officially endorsed way to borrow reach without aggregator risk.

- Weekly loop: check skip rate (fix hooks), retention curve (fix pacing), sends per reach (which memes get shared), and connected vs unconnected reach (is the account breaking out?). Re-cut the winners into new variations and test them as Trial Reels.

## 11. Penalties: Account Status, recommendation eligibility, AI labels, what makes content non-recommendable

### Takeaway
Instagram's **Recommendation Guidelines** are stricter than its Community Guidelines. Content that breaks them stays up but isn't shown to non-followers. **Account Status** (Settings) shows whether the account is recommendable and allows appeals. Triggers include recent Community Guidelines strikes, repeated recommendation-guideline violations, unoriginal or aggregator content, engagement bait, bought likes or followers, fact-checked false content, and (since **Aug 2026**) undisclosed photorealistic AI-generated personas. Using AI for editing or graphics does not require the label.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL]** The guidelines avoid recommending content that may be "low-quality, objectionable, or particularly sensitive" even when it isn't removed. They are stricter than Community Standards and enforced with automated detection. — [Instagram Help, Recommendations on Instagram](https://help.instagram.com/313829416281232/)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Non-recommendable account triggers: recent Community Guidelines violations; repeatedly posting content that violates the Recommendation Guidelines (then "none of their posts will be eligible for recommendation for a period of time"); repeated misleading growth tactics "such as purchasing 'likes'"; being banned from running ads; repeatedly posting content rated False or Altered by fact-checkers; and (for AI) repeatedly sharing photorealistic AI-generated people without the AI-generated profile label. — [Instagram Help](https://help.instagram.com/313829416281232/); [Instagram blog, Account Status](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-outages-and-account-status)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Account Status shows whether the account is eligible to be recommended in places like Search or Suggested Accounts, and you can request a review if you disagree. — [Instagram Help, Check your Account Status](https://help.instagram.com/338481628002750/)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Low-quality, non-recommendable content includes unoriginal repurposed content with immaterial edits, long captions unrelated to the content, and coordinated comment networks. — [Instagram Help](https://help.instagram.com/313829416281232/); [Meta Transparency Center, Types of content we demote](https://transparency.meta.com/features/approach-to-ranking/types-of-content-we-demote/)
- **[OFFICIAL]** Engagement bait and unoriginal content are also barred from monetization. — [Instagram Content Monetization Policies](https://help.instagram.com/2635536099905516)
- **[OFFICIAL, May 2026]** Instagram began rolling out "AI creator" labels for accounts posting AI-generated content. — [Social Media Today, "Instagram adds AI Creator labels"](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-adds-ai-creator-labels/819267/)
- **[OFFICIAL, Aug 31 2026]** The label was renamed "AI-generated profile." Accounts featuring AI-generated people without the label "could see their reach reduced," and labeled accounts avoid the penalty. People using AI to edit photos, polish captions, create graphics or make other creative tweaks don't need the label. — [TechCrunch, Aug 31 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/31/instagram-puts-new-limits-on-undisclosed-ai-profiles/); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-updates-tags-for-ai-profiles/829235/)
- **[OFFICIAL, Apr 2026]** Mosseri said growth hacks "sometimes work, usually don't, and when we find them, we usually shut them down." — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-debunks-popular-engagement-hack/816781/)
- **[REPORTED, Jun 2026, unverified]** A June 2026 wave of account bans tied to "account integrity" checks appeared on X trending. It was not verified by a primary source. — [X trending](https://x.com/i/trending/2064680074713223489)

### Inferences
- For emo, the main self-inflicted risks are: (1) reposting community or other memes as feed posts (aggregator classification); (2) "comment EMO to win/airdrop" style engagement bait; (3) buying followers or likes or joining engagement pods or comment networks; and (4) any photorealistic AI-generated "people." A cartoon mascot or pet character is not a "person." The AI-profile rule targets photorealistic AI humans, but clearly AI-made content can still get "AI info" labeling. Check Account Status weekly, especially early on.
- "Shadowban" folklore can now be checked directly against Account Status. If it shows recommendable, low reach is a content-performance issue, not a penalty.
- Crypto and financial-promotion recommendability is out of scope here (covered by another researcher), but it is likely the biggest Account Status risk for a memecoin-tied brand.

### Gaps
- Instagram does not publish the size or duration of reach reductions (for AI-profile non-disclosure or repeated violations, it says only "a period of time").
- Whether general "AI info" content labels (as opposed to the AI-generated profile label) reduce reach was not addressed in any 2026 source found. Neither Mosseri nor Instagram was found quantifying it.
