# Chain-Flagship Memecoin Comparables and Base Rates for Large Multiples (2023 to 2026-10-13)

> **How these notes were made, and what that means for the numbers:** Every direct page fetch failed during this session (DNS and proxy errors on arxiv, cointelegraph, decrypt, blockworks, fortune and substack). All figures below come from search-engine extracts of the cited pages, not from reading the full pages. Market-cap figures from price-aggregator pages (CoinGecko, Coinbase, Gemini, CryptoRank) are often undated snapshots. Where sources conflict, both figures are shown. Treat every number as approximate and re-check it against CoinGecko or CoinMarketCap history before publishing. Any multiple marked "(arithmetic)" was computed here from the cited figures.
>
> **Survivorship bias:** this comparison set is biased by construction. It is made of tokens that became famous. For every flagship listed there were dozens to hundreds of same-chain, same-season memes that reached about $1M and then went to zero. Several chains (Blast, Berachain, Sei, Aptos, Abstract) appear only as "no large meme found", because their failed flagship candidates were never covered.

## 1. Solana flagships (BONK, WIF, POPCAT, MEW and others)

### Takeaway
Solana produced the strongest flagship cohort of the cycle: BONK, WIF, POPCAT and MEW each reached roughly $1B to $4.7B. WIF and POPCAT were new tokens launched at the very start of Solana's late-2023 revival and passed through $1M on the way up within days. BONK was the exception. It was an incumbent "chain community coin" that had sat at about $17M to $35M in the quiet phase, then made about 120x over 13 months (arithmetic), driven by the chain revival plus Binance and Upbit listings.

### Inferences
- **Two archetypes.** (a) *New-launch flagships* such as WIF, POPCAT, MEW, Fartcoin and GOAT launched at or near the start of a meme wave and passed $1M within days or weeks. Their "from $1M" multiple was 1,000x or more (WIF about 4,700x if the Pluang figure is right). (b) *Incumbent community coin*: BONK went from about $17M to $35M in late October 2023 (the 101% weekly gain implies about $17M a week earlier) to $4.1B to $4.2B in November 2024, about 120x to 240x over 13 months (arithmetic). The incumbent path, with a token that already exists at a modest valuation before the chain's revival, is the closer analogue for an existing ~$1M token.
- **Two peaks.** Solana flagships peaked first in the Dec 2023 to Mar 2024 window, during the chain revival. They peaked again, higher, in Nov 2024 (BONK Nov 20, POPCAT Nov 17, MEW Nov 17) during the broad post-US-election rally. The largest peaks lined up with the overall crypto market as well as the chain's own cycle.
- CEX listings were the most consistent nearby catalyst for each leg: Binance for BONK (Dec 2023), Binance for WIF (Mar 2024, ATH 26 days later) and Upbit KRW for BONK (Nov 2024, ATH the same day).

### Gaps
- BONK's actual mid-2023 trough market cap was not found. Pull it from CoinGecko history.
- WIF's market cap on its Binance listing day, and an independent check of the Pluang "<$1M to >$350M" claim, were not found.
- BOME, MYRO, SLERF and other Solana flagship candidates that failed were not researched. This is a survivorship gap.

## 2. Base (BRETT, TOSHI, DEGEN, MIGGLES and others)

### Takeaway
Base's meme season (roughly Feb/Mar 2024 to Dec 2024) produced one $2B flagship (BRETT, Pepe's friend from Boy's Club in Base blue) and one roughly $1.5B "utility" meme (DEGEN, the Farcaster tipping token). Strong chain growth did **not** produce durable meme valuations. By 2026 snapshots, BRETT was about 97.5% below its ATH and TOSHI and DEGEN were in the tens of millions.

### Inferences
- BRETT's path (<$10M → ~$2B, about 200x in 101 days, then a larger ATH about nine months later) is the best documented "new chain, culture tie-in" flagship outcome after Solana. Note that BRETT launched just before the run and never lingered at ~$1M.
- Base's case suggests that chain usage growth (TVL, transactions, Coinbase distribution) did not by itself sustain meme valuations. Base memes followed the same roughly 97% peak-to-trough path as Solana's.
- TOSHI had the strongest tie-in to the "parent company" (the Coinbase CEO's cat) but did not become the largest. Culture tie-in helps, but did not decide the winner.

### Gaps
- No dated peak market caps were found for TOSHI, DEGEN or MIGGLES. The DEGEN $1.5B figure comes from a single source.
- No dated Base-meme-sector time series for 2025–2026 was found, so the "season ended" call rests on token-level snapshots.
- No source tied the Dencun upgrade (March 2024 fee cuts) to Base meme activity, so it is not cited.

## 4. Patterns of winners: earliest, culture tie-in, community, utility, or CEX listings?

### Takeaway
No single factor decides the winner, but the most consistent pattern across chains is **chain-identity tie-in + timing at the start of the chain's speculative wave + tier-1 CEX listings (Binance, Upbit, Coinbase) that bring outside buyers in**. Being the earliest meme was neither necessary nor sufficient. Explicit backing by the foundation or exchange (Avalanche Foundation for COQ; CZ and Binance Alpha for BNB memes) acted as a strong accelerant. "Builder" memes with products (BONK, DEGEN, PURR) did not reach higher peaks than pure memes, but BONK held up better in the drawdown and had a product-driven rebound.

### Cited Findings
- **Chain/parent identity tie-ins among winners:** BONK, the Solana "community dog" airdropped to Solana users ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/118295)); BRETT, Pepe's friend in Base blue, the first Base meme to $1B ([Unchained](https://unchainedcrypto.com/?p=25807)); TOSHI, the Coinbase CEO's cat ([nft now](https://nftnow.com/guides/base-memecoins-guide/)); PURR, Hyperliquid's first spot token and cat mascot ([CoinEx](https://www.coinex.com/en/academy/detail/1750-breaking-down-purr-the-first-spot-launch-on-hyperliquid-l1)); Broccoli, CZ's dog ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/305834/binance-founder-cz-dog-name-meme-coins)); 币安人生 "Binance Life" ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/343290/bnb-meme-season-arrives-binance-cz-coins-get-hot)).
- **Earliest was not necessary:** WIF launched Nov 20, 2023, nearly a year after BONK, and passed it ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/220417/solana-new-top-dog-meme-coin-dogwifhat-flips-bonk?amp=1)). BRETT launched "days" before its run and passed earlier Base memes such as TOSHI ([Blockworks](https://blockworks.co/news/base-memecoin-brett-solana-bonk)).
- **CEX listings as catalysts:** BONK (Binance, Dec 2023) ([AMBCrypto](https://ambcrypto.com/tracing-bonks-journey-as-it-becomes-2023s-top-gainer/)); WIF (Binance listing Mar 5, 2024, ATH Mar 31) ([Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/dogwifhat-meme-coin)); BONK (Upbit KRW Nov 20, 2024, ATH the same day) ([FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/cryptocurrencies/news/bonk-reaches-fresh-all-time-high-propelled-by-upbit-listing-hefty-token-burn-202411201358)); BRETT (Binance futures August 2024, +38%) ([The Informer Post](https://theinformerpost.beehiiv.com/p/coinbase-ecosystem-memecoin-breaks-out-after-earning-sudden-binance-futures-listing)); Binance Life (Binance Alpha listing, $0 to $150M in 3 days) ([ChainCatcher](https://www.chaincatcher.com/en/article/2210986)).
- **Foundation backing:** the Avalanche Foundation's Culture Catalyst purchases. COQ was more than 74% of a roughly $541M basket of five foundation-held memes ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/282714/avalanche-foundation-reveals-five-community-coins-it-holds)).
- **Builder memes:** BONK's LetsBonk launchpad passed pump.fun's revenue in July 2025 and BONK rose about 145% in a month on revenue-funded buybacks ([BTC-Echo](https://www.btc-echo.de/schlagzeilen/letsbonk-ueberholt-pump-fun-bei-einnahmen-und-volumen-sorgt-fuer-bonk-rallye-210889/); [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/pump-plummets-while-bonk-rallies-on-new-buyback-mechanism)), but the lead flipped back within weeks ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/365737/pump-fun-reclaims-letsbonk)). DEGEN, with real tipping utility on Farcaster, reached about $1.5B ([TechFlow](https://techflowpost.com/en-US/article/17129)) but later fell to about $62M ([CoinGecko](https://coingecko.com/en/categories/base-meme-coins)). PURR, with fee burns and venue-native liquidity, still fell about 94% peak to trough ([CoinMarketCap](https://coinmarketcap.com/currencies/purr)).
- **Fair launch vs insider launch:** POPCAT was a fair launch with burned LP ([Phemex](https://phemex.com/academy/what-is-popcat-token-solana-meme-coin-guide)). BRETT was criticized for a whitelist-only first hour and insider concentration ([Unchained](https://unchainedcrypto.com/?p=25807)). Both reached about $2B, so launch fairness did not visibly decide peak size.

### Inferences
- **Ranking of factors, from the comparables (judgment, small n):** (1) the chain or exchange's own speculative wave arriving, which is necessary; (2) identity tie-in to the chain or its founder, present in most flagships; (3) tier-1 CEX listings, which line up most tightly with each leg's peak; (4) community and fair launch, which help but do not decide; (5) utility or products, which mainly affect *survival after the peak* rather than peak size.
- **Pure vs builder:** pure memes (WIF, POPCAT) rose to peak fastest and reached higher peaks relative to age. Among the Solana flagships, BONK (the most "builder" one, with bots, integrations and a launchpad with burns) had the shallowest documented drawdown (about −85% vs about −94% for WIF and about −98% for POPCAT by late 2025 or 2026) and the only clear product-driven rebound (July 2025). n=3, so this is suggestive only.

### Gaps
- No quantitative study comparing "utility" vs "pure" meme returns was found.
- There is no systematic dataset of losing flagship candidates on each chain (for example, early Base memes that faded, or Blast and Berachain memes), so these patterns are inferred from survivors.

## 5. Drawdowns after the season

### Takeaway
Flagship memes typically fell **85% to 99%** from peak, usually within **4 to 13 months**, and very few regained their highs. The best systematic evidence (Coin Metrics, 150 CEX-listed memes) found a median of about 17 days from first trade to ATH, 81% falling at least 90%, a median of about 370 days to a 95% drawdown, and only 3.3% ever regaining their ATH.

- **Rule of thumb for scenario modeling:** after a flagship's season peak, expect about −60% to −75% within 1 to 4 months and about −90% to −98% within 12 to 18 months, unless a new market-wide wave arrives. - For a ~$1M token, a 10x to 100x run that is not sold into is likely to give back most of the gain within a year. Base rates for "touched X" are much higher than for "held X".

## 6. Base rates: from ~$1M to $10M, $100M and $1B

### Takeaway
No study directly measures the conditional probability of going from $1M to $10M, $100M or $1B. Combining the available data points (pump.fun snapshot distributions, counts of $1B memes, Coin Metrics survival, CoinGecko failure counts) gives an order-of-magnitude estimate for a *generic* launchpad meme that touches about $1M: **reaching $10M at some point ≈ 5% to 10%, $100M ≈ 0.5% to 1%, $1B ≈ 0.05% to 0.1%**. For a token that is already a *leading* meme on a new chain *before* that chain's meme wave, the odds are plausibly several times higher at the 10x and 100x tiers: roughly 15% to 30%, 2% to 5%, and ≤0.5% respectively (judgment). These are "touched at least once" probabilities. Holding the gain is far rarer (see section 5).

### Cited Findings
- **pump.fun, 2024 snapshot:** 89 of nearly 2M pump.fun tokens (0.0045%) were above $1M market cap at once. About 0.3% of *graduated* tokens were above $1M. About 1.5% of launches graduated (about $69K market cap) — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/249107/fewer-than-100-pump-fun-tokens-above-1m-market-cap-amid-meme-coin-lull)
- **Graduation rates vary by method:** 0.63% of 655,770 tokens (Sep–Oct 2025, Marino et al.); 0.84% of 763,350 launches (April 2026); about 1.4% lifetime; and a 0.198% figure that was withdrawn as unreliable — [arXiv 2607.02823](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02823); [pumpfundata](https://pumpfundata.com/blog/pumpfun-graduation-rate-analysis); [TechFlow](https://www.techflowpost.com/en-US/article/19808); [arXiv 2602.14860](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.14860)
- **$1B pump.fun memes in 2024:** at least Fartcoin ($1.31B), GOAT ($1.3B) and PNUT (about $1.6B, weak source) — [Fortune](https://www.fortune.com/crypto/2024/12/19/fartcoin-1-billion-market-cap-memecoin-market-explodes); [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/fartcoin-pnut-wyac-silliest-memecoins-2024); [Hype Train](https://hype-train.beehiiv.com/p/two-new-coins-hit-1-billion-market-cap)
- Only about 97,000 of more than 7M pump.fun tokens kept liquidity above $1,000 — cited in search results around [Decrypt/NFT Evening](https://nftevening.com/pump-fun-deep-dive/) (secondary, unverified)
- **BDC Consulting (CoinGecko and CMC data):** only 5% of memecoins had a market cap above $10M in March 2024 — [BDC Consulting](https://bdc.consulting/insights/MarketResearch/memecoins)
- **CoinGecko:** about 11.6M tokens failed in 2025, 86.3% of all failures from 2021 to 2025. 53.2% of tokens listed on GeckoTerminal since 2021 have failed. Only *graduated* pump.fun tokens are counted — [Cointelegraph on CoinGecko](https://cointelegraph.com/news/crypto-project-failures-record-2025-memecoin-turbulence)
- **Solidus Labs:** about 98.6% to 98.7% of pump.fun tokens and 93% of Raydium pools show pump-and-dump or rug characteristics — [Solidus Labs](https://www.soliduslabs.com/reports/solana-rug-pulls-pump-dumps-crypto-compliance)
- **Chainalysis:** in 2022, about 1 in 4 evaluable new tokens (9,902 of 40,521) fell at least 90% in their first week. In 2023, 54% of Ethereum DEX-listed tokens met the pump-and-dump criteria — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/215205/54-of-tokens-appear-to-be-pump-and-dumps-but-theres-good-news); [Nasdaq/Decrypt](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/crypto-fraudsters-made-%2430-million-last-year-off-pump-and-dump-schemes%3A-report)
- **Coin Metrics** (survivors only): 3.3% regain their ATH, and 81% fall at least 90% — [Coin Metrics](https://coinmetrics.substack.com/p/state-of-the-network-issue-384)
- **Cross-chain three-month study:** 50.43% of Solana tokens showed price losses, with a median decline among losers of 26% — [arXiv 2507.01963](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.01963)
- **Unsourced claim seen in results:** "only 0.23% of meme coins stay above $1M for long" — [Analytics Insight](https://www.analyticsinsight.net/meme-coin/why-a-meme-coin-reaching-10-million-actually-matters) (origin unclear; low confidence)

### Inferences
- **How the generic estimate was built (assumption-heavy; label as inference):**
  1. pump.fun snapshot: about 89 tokens above $1M at any time in mid-2024. With the median token peaking about 17 days after launch (Coin Metrics), the set above $1M turns over roughly 15 to 25 times a year. That implies about 1,500 to 5,000 distinct pump.fun tokens touching $1M in a year like 2024.
  2. At least 3 to 6 pump.fun tokens touched $1B in 2024. 3–6 / 1,500–5,000 ≈ 0.06% to 0.4% for 1000x, with the lower end more likely once tokens that briefly touched $1M are counted.
  3. Market caps in crypto roughly follow a power law. With a Pareto exponent near 1 (an assumption, not sourced here), each 10x step up keeps about 10% of the tokens: about 10% reach $10M, about 1% reach $100M and about 0.1% reach $1B. This fits step 2 and the BDC snapshot (5% of listed memes above $10M).
  4. Result for a generic ~$1M meme: P(10x) ≈ 5% to 10%, P(100x) ≈ 0.5% to 1%, P(1000x) ≈ 0.05% to 0.1%.
- **Adjustment for $EMO-like situations (judgment):** being a top meme on a new L1 *before* its meme wave raises the odds that it captures flows if a wave comes. Evidence: incumbents BONK, TOSHI and PURR each made 10x+ when their chain's wave hit. But (a) about 40% of comparable new-chain scenes never produced a $100M meme, (b) most flagships were *new* tokens launched at the start of the wave rather than incumbents, and (c) Monad's top memes were only about $1M in the snapshots found. That gives P(10x) ≈ 15% to 30%, P(100x) ≈ 2% to 5%, P(1000x) ≈ ≤0.5%. All three depend heavily on whether Monad gets a Solana/Base-style meme season with tier-1 CEX listings for its memes.
- **Survivorship bias:** the case studies in sections 1 to 3 cover about 15 winners. Counting every same-season token that reached about $1M on those chains would push the realized 100x rate toward the low single-digit percent or below.

### Gaps
- No published cohort study gives "P($10M | touched $1M)" or the equivalent at $100M or $1B. The estimates above are derived, not measured. A Dune query on pump.fun or four.meme peak market caps by cohort would settle this.
- The total count of pump.fun-origin tokens that ever exceeded $100M or $1B in 2024–2025 was not found.
- The four.meme and BNB-season cohort distribution (how many tokens passed $1M, $10M or $100M in October 2025) was not found.

## 7. Leading indicators before each chain's meme season

### Takeaway

Memes giving back 30% in a day while most of the field stays under $50M (BNB, October 2025) — [CoinGape](https://coingape.com/robinhood-chains-noxa-crashes-after-receiving-12m-in-fees-top-memecoins-plunge-by-over-30/); [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/robinhood-chain-revenue-falls-83-percent/); [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/markets/bnb-chain-memecoins-take-30-tumble-is-binance-s-meme-rush-over)

### Inferences
- **Monad checklist for $EMO:** (1) a large airdrop or token event that leaves recipients with liquid MON and a reason to stay on-chain; (2) MON breaking out against BTC and ETH; (3) Monad Foundation or core-team members openly endorsing memes, or a foundation meme fund like Avalanche's; (4) the Monad launchpad (nad.fun) and DEX volume rising sharply, or Monad DEX volume passing a peer chain; (5) a tier-1 CEX listing a Monad meme. Without (2) to (5), the comparables suggest a ~$1M chain-meme stays small: Blast, Berachain, Sei, Aptos and Abstract all show this.
- Chain-level user growth alone (Base TVL and transactions) was a weaker predictor of meme durability than price-led speculation and exchange endorsement.

### Gaps
- No quantitative leading-indicator study (for example, new-wallet growth vs. later meme market cap across chains) was found. The indicators above are case-based.
- Base's Dencun fee drop (March 2024) and Hyperliquid's HYPE airdrop (Nov 29, 2024) are widely known but were not verified with a source in this pass.
