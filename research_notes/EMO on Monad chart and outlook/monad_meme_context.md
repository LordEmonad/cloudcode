## 1. MON: sale price, launch, price history, current valuation, and comparison with other L1 launches

### Takeaway
MON sold at $0.025 (Coinbase, Nov 17–22, 2025; $2.5B FDV), spiked to ~$0.049 around mainnet (late Nov 2025), bottomed at ~$0.0162 in early Feb 2026, and as of Oct 11–12, 2026 trades around $0.026 (~$290–307M market cap, ~$2.6B FDV). That is about flat vs. the ICO price and about 47% below ATH, after spending much of mid-2026 below the sale price. That is much weaker than Sui (multiples of its $0.10 sale price) or Hyperliquid (~20x+ from TGE), but far better than Berachain's ~90%+ collapse.

## 3. Category Labs / Monad Foundation: 2026 ships, catalysts, and the token unlock overhang

### Takeaway
2026 ships were mostly protocol performance work: MIP-8 storage pages, MIP-12 300ms blocks, RaptorCast work, and a post-quantum account-auth draft. Ecosystem additions included 1inch, Drake and an Aave V4 proposal, plus the $60M early-investor buyback in Aug 2026. The dominant upcoming event is the first major unlock on Nov 24, 2026: ~16.6–16.8B MON (team ~10.7B, investors ~4.9B, Category Labs treasury ~1B), roughly 1.4x the current circulating float, followed by ~1.16B MON per month.

### Cited Findings
- MIP-8 (page-encoded storage) is live on mainnet; it makes clustered/multi-slot storage access cheaper (cited as up to 98% gas cost cut). — [CoinMarketCap AI updates](https://coinmarketcap.com/cmc-ai/monad/latest-updates/)
- MIP-12 cut block times by reducing vote pacing from 400ms to 300ms and is reported live on mainnet (one source says Jul 23, 2026). An older CMC piece still calls it a proposal, so the date is unconfirmed. — [CoinMarketCap AI updates](https://coinmarketcap.com/cmc-ai/monad/latest-updates/); [Monad Apr–Jun 2026 highlights](https://monad.xyz/blog/monad-ecosystem-highlights-april-june-2026)
- MIP-10 (RaptorCast-related; validators vote without waiting for the full block) was still upcoming as of the April roundup. — [Monad Apr–Jun 2026 highlights](https://monad.xyz/blog/monad-ecosystem-highlights-april-june-2026)
- Aug 2026: Category Labs published a draft MIP for Flexible and Upgradeable Account Authentication, the first step of a post-quantum roadmap (authors Kushal Babel, Jan Camenisch). One source says a post-quantum roadmap was published Sep 28, 2026. — [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/monad-wallet-upgrade-quantum-resistant/); [Monad Aug highlights](https://monad.xyz/blog/monad-ecosystem-highlights-august-2026); [CoinStats](https://coinstats.app/ai/a/fundamental-analysis-monad)
- Category Labs published a threshold encryption paper and signaled a privacy feature. — [CoinMarketCap AI updates](https://coinmarketcap.com/cmc-ai/monad/latest-updates/)
- Aug 2026: $60M early-investor buyback completed; nearly all invitees declined. — [Crypto-Economy](https://crypto-economy.com/monad-60m-early-investor-exit-program-rejected/)
- Official tokenomics: investors have a four-year lock-up from mainnet with a one-year cliff, then equal 1/48 monthly unlocks. Team tokens are locked for the first year and released from the 1-year anniversary over the following three years. The schedule runs to Q4 2029. — [Monad tokenomics](https://www.monad.xyz/announcements/mon-tokenomics-overview); [Backpack Learn](https://learn.backpack.exchange/articles/monad-tokenomics)
- Nov 24, 2026 unlock: ~16.8B MON (≈10.7B team, 4.925B investors, 1B Category Labs treasury); Dropstab lists 16.62B. Afterwards, monthly unlocks of ~1.16B MON. — [CryptoTicker](https://cryptoticker.io/en/monad-mon-unlock-lock-up-expiry/); [Dropstab vesting](https://dropstab.com/coins/monad/vesting)
- Another source says allocations totaling "over 46 billion MON" begin unlocking in Nov 2026. This is the total that starts vesting, not a single-day release. — [StealthEX](https://stealthex.io/blog/monad-price-prediction-will-mon-coin-reach-1-soon/)
- BSC News frames the Nov 2026 unlock as Monad's "biggest test yet". — [BSC News on X](https://x.com/BSCNews/article/2099457787445313684)
- No 2026 Category Labs or Monad Foundation ecosystem fund announcement was found.

### Inferences
- 4.925B investor tokens = 25% of ~19.7B (12/48 months released at the cliff), which matches the official 1/48 schedule. That makes the investor figure credible.
- At ~$0.026, the Nov 24 unlock is ~$430–440M of newly transferable MON, against a ~$290–307M market cap and ~11–12B circulating supply. That is a large overhang. Ongoing ~1.16B/month (~$30M/month at current prices) adds persistent supply. Memecoins are typically priced in, or paired against, MON on nad.fun, so MON weakness around the unlock would directly hit MON-denominated meme liquidity and USD market caps.
- The near-total rejection of the $60M discounted exit could mean holders want to hold, or that the discount was unattractive. Analysts disagree, and the discount was undisclosed.

### Gaps
- No primary Category Labs post confirming MIP-12/MIP-10 mainnet dates, and no 2026 ecosystem fund or new CEX listing announcements found.
- Exact team allocation size and the post-cliff team release cadence are not verified from the primary tokenomics page snippet.

## 4. Monad memecoins: largest by market cap and volume, totals and trend, and where $EMO ranks

### Takeaway

CHOG is ~$1.5–1.8M (stale), early mascot memes like MOLANDAK are ~$5.5K (Sep 2026), and GeckoTerminal's Monad meme category showed only ~$5.4K daily volume in one undated snapshot. $EMO, at ~$0.85–0.91M market cap and 6,249 holders (late Sep 2026), plausibly ranks among the largest surviving Monad memes, but no source publishes a ranking or a total Monad meme market cap.

## 7. Chains with healthy TVL/price but dead meme scenes, and what drives a meme season

### Takeaway
Monad is itself a current example of the pattern: record DeFi TVL (~$1B) and DEX volume in Aug–Sep 2026 alongside a collapsed meme scene (sub-$2M leaders, ~12K daily active addresses). Berachain and Sui show the same decoupling. Sources point to the same meme-season drivers: a hot, cheap launchpad; trading bot/terminal support; a CEX listing pipeline; founder or exchange-brand endorsement; and social attention. TVL is not one of them.

### Cited Findings
- Berachain: TVL fell from $3.35B to ~$393M within a year. Its meme ecosystem was "mostly infrastructure" with no standout memecoin, weak UIs, thin liquidity and small communities, despite a meme-branded identity and a $4M Honeypot incentive program meant to "power memecoin season". — [BlockEden](https://blockeden.xyz/blog/2026/01/26/berachain-one-year-later-proof-of-liquidity-reality-check/); [Gate Learn](https://www.gate.com/learn/articles/promising-meme-projects-in-the-berachain-ecosystem/8137); [HackerNoon](https://hackernoon.com/honeypot-finance-set-to-power-memecoin-season-on-berachain-with-$4m-in-incentives)
- Sui: in late 2024 TVL and price rose while daily active wallets showed no significant increase, implying growth from a small set of large holders. Attribution within search results is uncertain. — [VanEck Sui vs. Aptos](https://www.vaneck.com/us/en/insights/blogs/digital-assets/sui-vs-aptos-competitive-analysis-and-price-prediction/); [Pashi's Substack](https://pashipas.substack.com/p/blockchain-briefs-sui)
- Monad: TVL record with thin net inflows (Aug 2026); native apps struggled while Uniswap and Curve dominated early activity. — [Yellow TVL](https://yellow.com/news/monad-868m-tvl-mon-demand); [Yellow apps](https://yellow.com/news/native-monad-applications-struggle-as-uniswap-curve-dominate-early-ecosystem-activity)
- Launchpad to DEX to CEX pipeline: most memes start on a bonding curve, migrate to a DEX on graduation, and only a small share reach CEX spot. Four.meme ties CEX-listing rewards to market-cap milestones (e.g., $44,444). — [Gate Learn: Four.meme](https://www.gate.com/learn/articles/xploring-four-meme-the-bnb-chain-memecoin-launchpad/8692); [WEEX meme guide 2026](https://www.weex.com/learn/articles/meme-coin-guide-opportunities-risks-strategy-2026-16650)
- Cheap launches and bots: a Robinhood Chain launchpad minting ~25K tokens/day depends on very low cost per attempt. Fees fell ~31% after free gas ended, and most fee-generating usage ran through the Pons launchpad and trading bots. — [CryptoTicker: Robinhood Chain memecoins](https://cryptoticker.io/en/robinhood-chain-memecoins-explained/)
- GMGN chain-selection signals: back-to-back new launches with real volume, smart-money wallet activity, chain DEX volume, bridge inflows, and stablecoin supply. GMGN is a trading terminal, so its framing is self-interested. — [GMGN Blog](https://gmgn.ai/blog/how-to-choose-a-chain-for-meme-trading/)
- Galaxy Research: in meme economies, launchpads, DEXs and trading bots capture most revenue, while most traders lose money on average (Solana-era analysis). — reported in [MEXC News](https://www.mexc.com/news/117225)
- Leader/brand endorsement as an ignition: CZ's and Yi He's posts drove the Oct 2025 BSC season. — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/bnb-meme-season-arrives-binance-210125348.html); [LBank](https://www.lbank.com/explore/bianrensheng-how-binance-life-became-a-global-crypto-meme)
- Social attention leads price; memes are tied to X, Telegram and group chats. — [WEEX meme guide 2026](https://www.weex.com/learn/articles/meme-coin-guide-opportunities-risks-strategy-2026-16650)

### Inferences
- Monad today has some ingredients: a native launchpad (nad.fun), Coinbase-distributed MON (a retail onramp), GMGN/DexScreener coverage, and NADS on MEXC. It lacks the ignition seen elsewhere: a leader or brand push, a launchpad-to-CEX listing pipeline, and retail daily actives (~12K vs. >150K at TGE). Record DeFi TVL is not translating into meme flow, matching the Berachain and Sui pattern.
- For a Monad meme like $EMO, upside from a chain-wide "meme season" depends on a Monad-specific catalyst (e.g., a foundation or Category Labs push, a nad.fun incentive program, a CEX listing wave) rather than on the macro tape. The Nov 24, 2026 MON unlock is a known headwind in the near term.
