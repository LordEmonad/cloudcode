# Make emo famous before you mention $EMO

Run TikTok and Instagram as an entertainment channel for a character, emo and his Emogotchi pets, and keep the token off both: no contract address and no "buy" language in bios, captions or comments, with token details on your own website one click away. That choice does more for growth and account survival than anything else here, because both platforms test every new post on strangers and judge it on its own numbers, and both have rules that hit memecoin promotion directly. TikTok's guidelines in force since September 24, 2026 reportedly add "high-risk financial services" to the not-allowed list, and Meta bans posts that cite past returns to imply future ones and giveaways of anything with cash value for engagement. The best-documented crypto IP success, Pudgy Penguins, built its audience with crypto-free character content and added a token later, and every viral memecoin of 2024–25 rode attention that already existed somewhere else. With an $800K market cap, about 6,300 holders and an Emogotchi airdrop that reached about 82k recipients but engaged only about 0.2% of them, emo's bottleneck is attention and activation, not token distribution; because passkey wallets let a viewer adopt a pet without a seed phrase, the call to action is "adopt a sad pet", not "buy". Day to day, that means one short vertical video a day on TikTok and four to seven posts a week on Instagram from a watermark-free master library, a backlog dripped over 12 or more weeks, one weekly scheduling batch, and about four to five hours a week. Treat the numbers with care: every web page behind this guide was read through search-engine extracts rather than full fetches, most benchmarks are vendor data showing correlation rather than cause, and the case studies only cover accounts that succeeded.

## TLDR: the ten rules that matter most

1. **Run both accounts as a character channel, not a token channel.** emo and the Emogotchi pets are the show. Don't post tickers, charts, prices or "buy" calls. Token details live on your own website.
2. **Keep the contract address out of both bios.** The bio says who emo is and invites people to adopt a sad pet. Its link goes to your own hub page, which holds the CA, the chain, the official links and a risk line.
3. **Never trade tokens for engagement.** "Follow, like and tag 3 friends to win $EMO" breaks Meta's spam rules and TikTok's For You rules, and it looks exactly like a wallet-drainer scam.
4. **Make vertical video the engine.**
   - TikTok: 6–20 s loopable memes, plus 30–90 s lore and gameplay.
   - Instagram: 15–60 s Reels, plus meme carousels.
   - Both: open with the joke as on-screen text in frame one, burn in captions, and end on a frame that loops.
5. **Optimize one number per platform.** On TikTok it's how long people keep watching. On Instagram it's sends per reach, meaning how often people DM the post to a friend. Write every meme to be sent.
6. **Post daily on TikTok and 4–7 times a week on Instagram, and never skip a week.** Release the backlog gradually over 12+ weeks instead of dumping it.
7. **Post only original, watermark-free work.** Export from clean masters, and never upload a TikTok download to Instagram. Don't let reposts of other people's memes become most of your feed.
8. **Schedule evergreen posts; post trends by hand.** Do all scheduling in one weekly session. Trending sounds can only be added inside the apps.
9. **Treat the comment section as your writers' room.** Reply within the first hour, turn the best comments into videos, and run multi-day lore arcs that send people into Emogotchi.
10. **Review weekly, decide monthly, and judge the whole effort at 12 weeks.** Make more versions of your top fifth of posts, put new hooks on the middle, and drop any format that flops twice.

**How to read the evidence.**

- *(official)*: a platform's own policy or executive statement.
- *(vendor data)*: a large dataset from a social-media tool company. It shows correlation, not cause.
- *(thin)*: a blog post, a single source or an anecdote.
- *(conflicting)*: sources disagree.
- *(inference)*: a recommendation drawn from the evidence rather than stated by a source.

Every page was read through search-engine extracts, so exact figures mean "as reported".

## Both algorithms test each post on strangers, then judge it alone

TikTok's only detailed official explanation of its For You feed is still its 2020 newsroom post, and the core of it holds. The feed ranks each video on three groups of signals:

- **Viewer interactions:** likes, shares, comments, follows and, above all, whether people finish watching.
- **Video information:** captions, sounds and hashtags.
- **Device and locale:** weighted lower than the other two.

The same post says **follower count and past hits are not direct ranking factors**. It also says the feed avoids showing two videos in a row from the same creator or with the same sound ([TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you/)) *(official)*. Everything more specific is practitioner folklore with no TikTok source. That includes the "200–500 viewer test batch", "70% completion unlocks the next wave" and fixed orderings of shares over saves over likes ([cut.pro](https://cut.pro/en/blog/tiktok-stuck-at-200-views-2026)) *(thin)*.

One structural change matters in 2026. Under the TikTok USDS joint venture formed on January 22, 2026, Oracle is retraining a licensed copy of the recommendation model on US data ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/computing/tiktok/the-deal-to-keep-tiktok-in-the-us-is-now-finalized-here-are-5-things-you-need-to-know)). No primary reporting yet says how ranking changed, so 2025 benchmarks may not carry over. The For You feed drives about **72.7% of TikTok views** in Metricool's 2026 dataset of 2.3M posts ([Metricool](https://metricool.com/press-release-tiktok-study-2026/)) *(vendor data)*.

Instagram is more explicit. Adam Mosseri, Instagram's head, has repeatedly named **watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach** as the top three signals. Likes count slightly more for reaching followers, and sends (DM shares) count slightly more for reaching non-followers ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/)) *(official)*. He restated this in May 2026 and told creators to compare connected (follower) reach with unconnected (non-follower) reach ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-engagement-rates-provide-insight-into-reach/821170/)). Each new Reel is first shown to a small audience of non-followers, and the best performers get wider distribution ([Instagram Creators blog](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/helping-creators-of-all-sizes-break-through)). His September 2026 video series added four principles, according to coverage of it ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-shares-algorithm-insights-and-posting-tips/831326/)):

- **Originality:** accounts that post their own work are favored over reposters.
- **Recency:** newer posts carry significant weight.
- **"Breaking content":** small creators are auditioned to non-followers.
- **Model size:** named in coverage, but its meaning for creators is unclear.

Two of Mosseri's points save a solo operator time:

- Stories are ranked almost entirely for existing followers and are "less optimal for discovery" ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-stories-ranking-factors-2025/738541/)).
- You don't need to engage with other accounts in your niche to raise your own reach ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-mosseri-says-edits-reach-boost-engagement-myths/757268/)).

| | TikTok | Instagram |
|---|---|---|
| Stated ranking inputs *(official)* | Finishing videos, shares, comments, likes, follows; captions, sounds, hashtags | Watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach |
| The number you optimize | Average % watched and 3-second hold | Sends per reach and skip rate (first 3 s) |
| How a new post is tested | Small first audience; size unpublished | Small non-follower audience, widened if above average |
| Role of follower count | Not a direct factor | Not a gate; small creators are auditioned to strangers |
| What gets demoted | Unoriginal or watermarked clips, GIF-only clips, undisclosed self-promotion | Reposts and aggregator accounts, other apps' watermarks, engagement bait |

This is good news for a brand-new account. Every post is its own lottery ticket, and a first video from emo can travel as far as one from a big account if strangers watch it through and pass it on. Diversity rules also mean that flooding near-identical videos won't stack reach from the same viewers *(inference)*.

There is one emo-specific caveat. TikTok's For You eligibility standards list "sadness" among themes that are fine occasionally but get "interrupted" when a viewer sees them in clusters ([TikTok FYF standards](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/fyf-standards/?enter_method=left_navigation)) *(official)*. That rule spreads such content out for each viewer; it doesn't penalize the account. Pou, the orange alien from a 2012 mobile game, shows that ironic sadness travels: its hashtag passed 5B views as a vehicle for loneliness jokes ([Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91028661/pou-orange-alien-tiktok)). Keep emo comedic and self-aware, and stay away from literal depression or self-harm content.

## Short looping video carries reach; carousels earn engagement

### TikTok: run two lanes of length

TikTok publishes no length or retention benchmark, and third-party data conflicts depending on which metric you look at:

- Completion falls steeply after about 10 seconds in one small sample ([SocialBu](https://socialbu.com/blog/ideal-lenghth-of-tiktok-videos)) *(thin)*.
- Buffer reportedly found more reach for videos over 60 seconds, relayed secondhand ([Quso.ai](https://quso.ai/blog/best-video-length-for-tiktok)) *(conflicting)*.
- TikTok's Creator Rewards Program pays only for original videos **over one minute** ([ShortSync](https://www.shortsync.app/resources/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-2026)).

The answer is two lanes:

- **6–20 second loopable memes** to test jokes on strangers.
- **30–90 second lore episodes or Emogotchi gameplay** for watch time and, later, monetization.

Video beats photo carousels decisively on TikTok views. Metricool's 2026 study found video drew about **5x the views and 6x the interactions** of carousels ([Metricool](https://metricool.com/press-release-tiktok-study-2026/)). Buffer's 45M-post study put median TikTok engagement at **3.39% for video versus 1.92% for photos** ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/data-best-content-format-social-media/)) *(vendor data)*. A 2025 dataset found the reverse on engagement rate (carousels 81% higher, but with 33% fewer shares) ([Metricool trends](https://metricool.com/tiktok-trends/)) *(conflicting)*. The Chill Guy meme's November 2024 resurgence also ran on TikTok photo slideshows ([Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/my-new-character-his-whole-deal-is-hes-a-chill-guy-that-lowkey-doesnt-give-a-fuck)). So keep slideshows as a secondary format.

Static meme images should become motion. TikTok's guidelines list "short clips made from GIFs only" and clips showing "someone else's watermark or logo" as ineligible for the For You feed ([TikTok Community Guidelines, 2025 snapshot](https://github.com/OpenTermsArchive/contrib-versions/blob/main/TikTok/Community%20Guidelines.md)) *(official)*.

Retention benchmarks are vendor-grade. One vendor claims a 62.4% median 3-second hold across 50k videos, and says 60%+ is needed to win wider distribution ([Retensis](https://retensis.com/blog/tiktok-retention-rate-benchmarks-2026)) *(thin)*. Use such numbers only until your own first 10–20 posts give you a baseline.

### Instagram: 15–60 second Reels plus carousels

Reels between 3 seconds and 3 minutes are eligible for recommendation. Longer uploads are allowed, but Instagram says they "won't be recommended to new audiences" ([Instagram](https://about.instagram.com/features/reels)) *(official)*. Instagram's own September 2026 guide calls **15–60 seconds the sweet spot** for most creative formats. It also says to add captions (subtitles) even when you already have text overlays ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-reels-creation-tips-in-new-guide/831551/)). Socialinsider's study of 6M Reels from January–June 2026 found that 45–60 s Reels had the highest engagement and median views, and that Reels under 30 s underperformed ([Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-length/)) *(vendor data; business accounts, not meme pages)*.

In practice, an Instagram meme Reel should usually run longer than its TikTok twin: stack two to four of your own beats, or extend a skit to 15–45 s *(inference)*. Mosseri has said Reels that are watermarked, low-resolution, muted, bordered or **mostly text** get less reach ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-answers-creator-questions/744813/)). So put the character on screen, not just a caption over a background.

The opening seconds feed **skip rate**, the share of viewers who scroll away in the first three seconds, which is now a core Insights metric. Socialinsider's 2026 benchmark averages about **65.5% skip for accounts with 1K–5K followers** ([Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram)).

Carousels are Instagram's engagement format. Buffer's 2026 data shows:

- **Reach:** Reels reach about **2.25x** as many people as single images.
- **Engagement per reach:** carousels lead at 6.90%, ahead of single images at 4.44% and Reels at 3.31% ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/data-best-content-format-social-media/)) *(vendor data)*.

Socialinsider finds Reels lead on reach only for accounts under 50K followers ([Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-statistics/)). That makes Reels your growth engine and carousels your engagement engine for now. Carousels also get a second chance: when someone doesn't swipe, Instagram often shows them slide 2 later ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/ig-chief-recommends-posting-carousels-improve-reach/730232/)) *(official)*, and carousels with music can appear in the Reels tab. Make slide 1 a strong standalone meme and slide 2 a second hook. Export images at 3:4 (1080×1440), which Instagram now supports natively ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-adds-support-phone-camera-aspect-ratio-images/749205/)).

### Hooks, captions, sound and hashtags

The hook is the joke's setup, written as on-screen text in the first frame so it reads with the sound off. Instagram's own workflow advice is blunt: "If viewers are dropping off at the 4-second mark, the hook needs work" ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-tips-on-video-creation/827172/)). End on a frame that cuts cleanly back to the start, so auto-replay counts as a rewatch *(inference; no source quantifies a loop bonus)*.

**Search.** On TikTok, captions, sounds and hashtags are official ranking inputs. Practitioners report that spoken words and on-screen text are indexed for search, so say and show the keyword ([usefastlane](https://www.usefastlane.ai/blog/tiktok-seo)) *(thin)*. Metricool found that hashtagged TikToks get about **5% more views**, and that posts asking a question get **26% more comments** ([Metricool](https://metricool.com/press-release-tiktok-study-2026/)).

**Hashtags.** Use **3–5 specific hashtags** on both platforms. Instagram has capped posts at five since December 2025, and Mosseri says hashtags help categorize a post rather than boost it ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/)) *(official)*. "emo" collides with the huge emo-music and emo-fashion niche. That brings search volume, but also viewers who came for something else. Pair #emo with a tag that defines your niche, such as #emomemes, #emogotchi or #virtualpet.

**Audio.** No large study compares trending sounds with original audio. A single-creator Buffer test found the trending-audio version beat a voiceover by about 24% ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/)) *(thin)*. Music licensing depends on what the post does, not only on the account type. TikTok Business accounts are limited to the Commercial Music Library (a set of pre-cleared tracks) ([Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-business-vs-personal/)), and promotional posts that use chart songs carry copyright risk on any account ([Soundstripe](https://www.soundstripe.com/blogs/tiktok-music-licensing-rules)). Use trending sounds on pure entertainment posts, and original or commercial-library audio on posts that push Emogotchi.

## Steady daily posting beats bursts, and the clock barely matters

Buffer's study of 11.4M TikTok posts found the biggest lift comes from moving from one post a week to two to five. Views per post rise about **17% at 2–5 a week, 29% at 6–10 and 34% at 11+**. Median views per post stay roughly flat, though, so extra posts mostly buy extra chances at a breakout ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-should-you-post-on-tiktok/); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/study-shows-2-5-weekly-tiktoks-deliver-biggest-view-increase/558641/)) *(vendor data)*.

On Instagram, Buffer links 3–5 posts a week to more than double the follower growth of 1–2 posts, and 6–9 a week to about 3.7x, with diminishing returns ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/)). Instagram itself says its fastest-growing creators post **10 or more Reels a month** ([Instagram Creators FAQ](https://creators.instagram.com/faq?locale=en_US)) *(official)*.

Consistency beats volume. Accounts that posted in 20 or more of 26 weeks saw about **450% more engagement per post** than accounts that posted in four or fewer ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/consistent-posting-study)). All of this is correlational, since active creators also tend to be better creators, but no evidence found says more posting hurts.

The cadence that follows *(inference)*:

- **TikTok:** one video a day, with a floor of three to five a week.
- **Instagram:** four to seven feed posts a week. Aim for a Reel a day when the backlog allows, at least 10–15 Reels a month, plus two or three carousels a week.
- **Stories:** two or three a week, optional, aimed at existing followers.
- **Spacing:** leave several hours between posts so each gets its own test window *(thin)*.
- **Patience:** hold the cadence for 8–12 weeks before drawing conclusions.

The arithmetic matters. At seven TikToks and five to seven Instagram posts a week, mostly cut from the same masters, a 12-week run uses roughly 90–100 unique pieces. If the backlog is smaller, drop to five TikToks and four Instagram posts a week rather than running dry. Don't dump the backlog. Burst-posting gains little per post, makes the account look mass-produced, and on Instagram can push reposted material over the aggregator line covered below.

Timing is a minor lever. Mosseri says there is "no direct correlation" between posting time and reach ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-answers-creator-questions/744813/)) *(official)*. TikTok best-time studies conflict: Buffer's is reported as favoring weekends and Monday, Sprout's as Tuesday–Wednesday afternoons ([eClincher summary](https://www.eclincher.com/articles/best-time-to-post-on-tiktok-in-2026-what-the-data-really-says)) *(conflicting)*. A TikTok post's useful life is about 10 days ([Metricool](https://metricool.com/tiktok-trends/)). Start with evenings and weekends in your audience's main time zone, likely US. Switch to your own "followers active" data once it exists, and above all be online for the first hour after each post.

**Flops.** No official source says deleting videos hurts. The defensible habit is to set flops to private rather than mass-delete, and never to re-upload the same file. Re-cut it with a new hook instead, which makes it a new video ([Orange Trail](https://orangetrail.io/blog/what-is-the-tiktok-shadowban)) *(thin)*.

**Burnout.** 52% of creators report career burnout, with creative fatigue and workload the top causes ([Billion Dollar Boy](https://www.billiondollarboy.com/news/over-half-of-creators-face-burnout)). The batch routine at the end of this guide exists to prevent that.

## Setup checklists: personal on TikTok, Creator on Instagram, no CA anywhere

### The bio question: leave the contract address off, and the ticker too for now

Many memecoin accounts put the ticker and contract address (CA) in the bio. The documented examples, though, sit mainly on X, such as an AI character whose X profile was nothing but a Pump.fun CA ([Medium](https://fernandocomet.medium.com/jean-philanthrope-ai-influencer-frenchman-with-a-mustache-virality-and-memecoin-b7853a3d8ec2)). On TikTok the common pattern is "group link in bio" funnels to off-platform chats ([TikTok Discover](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/meme-fi-coin-clan)) *(thin)*. The researchers could not open TikTok or Instagram profiles directly, so it is unverified what large memecoin brands show there today. The strongest crypto-IP case points the other way. Pudgy Penguins keeps its token behind a separate microsite with its own handles ([PENGU site](https://pengu.pudgypenguins.com/)), and CoinDesk describes its model as winning users through mainstream channels first ([CoinDesk Research](https://www.coindesk.com/research/pudgy-penguins-a-new-blueprint-for-tokenized-culture)).

No rule on either platform mentions contract addresses, and no report was found of an account banned only for having one in its bio. So the risk is reasoned from policy text, not measured *(inference)*. Even so, the text is pointed:

- **TikTok's definition of "trade"** includes "posting links… or directing people off-platform".
- **TikTok's regulated-goods rule** bans marketing or "providing access to" high-risk goods and services.
- **TikTok can ban accounts** "dedicated to… the unauthorized trading of… high-risk goods" ([TikTok Community Guidelines, 2026 snapshot](https://github.com/OpenTermsArchive/pga-versions/blob/main/TikTok/Community%20Guidelines.md)) *(official)*.
- **Instagram's Account Status** can flag "components of your profile" as breaking its recommendation guidelines. An account found ineligible has none of its content recommended ([Instagram Help via Intellicoach](https://intellicoach.ai/blog/instagram-not-eligible-for-recommendations-non-followers)).

A CA does save crypto-native viewers a step, and it gives them a canonical address that impersonators can't fake. Your website does that job better. List the official handles on DEX Screener, which verifies project social links through official channels ([Smithii](https://smithii.io/en/add-social-media-link-dexscreener/)), so the canonical CA stays checkable. Meanwhile, the bio stays out of the way of the recommendation engine that is the account's entire growth path.

| Tier | Bio and content | Risk | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Name "emo"; bio about the character and Emogotchi; link to your own hub page holding the CA, the chain (Monad), official links and a risk line; no ticker in captions | Lowest | **Use this at launch** |
| 2 | Tier 1 plus "$EMO on Monad" in the bio, no CA; occasional properly disclosed game posts | Moderate | Acceptable later if you decide holder conversion is worth it; watch Account Status after the change |
| 3 | Ticker and full CA in bio, "buy" or "100x" captions, charts or profit screenshots, token giveaways, DEX or wallet-connect links, coordinated holder raids | Highest; each element maps to a written rule | **Never** |

The CA itself (0x81A224F8A62f52BdE942dBF23A56df77A10b7777, Monad) belongs on the hub page, next to a short honest risk line such as "memecoin, no utility promise, can go to zero". Don't create a separate $EMO token account on TikTok or Instagram. Keep token community and announcements on X, Telegram and the website. TikTok explicitly prohibits using a second account to get around restrictions on the first ([TikTok Community Guidelines, 2025 snapshot](https://github.com/OpenTermsArchive/contrib-versions/blob/main/TikTok/Community%20Guidelines.md)).

### TikTok setup checklist

- [ ] **Account type: personal, not Business.** Business accounts only get the Commercial Music Library and are excluded from Creator Rewards ([Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-business-vs-personal/); [TTCalculator](https://ttcalculator.net/learn/creator-rewards-program/)). Some guides say native scheduling needs a "Creator or Business" account ([Hopper HQ](https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/how-to-schedule-tiktok-posts-desktop-mobile/)) *(conflicting)*. If TikTok Studio on desktop doesn't offer scheduling, use a third-party scheduler rather than switching to Business.
- [ ] **Handle and avatar:** the same handle on TikTok, Instagram, X and Telegram, and an avatar of emo's face that reads at thumbnail size.
- [ ] **Name and bio:** display name "emo", not "$EMO". Bio in one line, for example "sad lil guy 🖤 adopt his pets → Emogotchi".
- [ ] **Link:** a clickable bio link reportedly needs 1,000 followers on personal accounts ([Stan Store](https://stan.store/blog/tiktok-link-bio-requirements-2026-guide/)) *(conflicting)*. Until then, type the domain as plain text. Point it to your own hub page, never to a DEX or wallet-connect page.
- [ ] **2FA:** turn it on with an authenticator app, not SMS. Hijacked celebrity accounts that pushed fake memecoins were cleaned up, and one was disabled outright ([Mitrade](https://www.mitrade.com/insights/news/live-news/article-3-1063899-20250823)).
- [ ] **Pinned videos:** pin three "start here" videos: the lore intro, your best meme, and an adopt-a-pet clip. Three is the commonly cited pin limit *(thin)*. Group lore episodes into a playlist.
- [ ] **Disclosure toggle:** TikTok requires disclosure when "promoting your own business, product, or service" ([TikTok Community Guidelines, 2026 snapshot](https://github.com/OpenTermsArchive/pga-versions/blob/main/TikTok/Community%20Guidelines.md)). Turn on the content disclosure ("Your brand") toggle on posts that ask people to go play Emogotchi. Never mention the token in those posts.
- [ ] **Later milestones:**
  - 1,000 followers: bio link and LIVE (the most-cited threshold) ([OneStream](https://onestream.live/blog/how-to-get-live-access-on-tiktok/)).
  - 10,000 followers plus 100,000 views in 30 days: Creator Rewards, for original videos over a minute on personal accounts ([TTCalculator](https://ttcalculator.net/learn/creator-rewards-program/)).

### Instagram setup checklist

- [ ] **Account type:** switch to a public **Creator** account with the category "Digital creator" or "Entertainment". Professional accounts can see Account Status. Only professional accounts of owners aged 18+ can have their public posts indexed by search engines like Google ([Instagram Help](https://help.instagram.com/147542625391305/)). Business accounts are generally limited to commercially licensed audio ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-account-type/)).
- [ ] **Name field:** include searchable keywords, for example "emo | sad memes & Emogotchi". Search matches usernames, names, bios and captions ([Later](https://later.com/blog/instagram-seo/)).
- [ ] **Bio:** one line saying what the account is. No CA, and no ticker at launch.
- [ ] **Links:** you get up to five ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/04/18/instagram-takes-on-linktree-and-others-with-support-for-up-to-5-links-in-bio/)):
  1. Play Emogotchi, UTM-tagged.
  2. The emo hub page, which holds the CA and official links.
  3. Optionally, the X or Telegram community.
- [ ] **Highlights** as a "start here" menu: who is emo, play Emogotchi, best memes, and a safety notice ("we never DM first, never ask for seed phrases, no surprise airdrops").
- [ ] **2FA** with an authenticator app. Check **Settings → Account Status** weekly; it shows whether you are recommendable and lets you appeal ([Instagram Help](https://help.instagram.com/338481628002750/)).
- [ ] **Trial Reels:** look for the toggle from day one. Trial Reels go to non-followers only, and Instagram says they raised non-follower Reels reach by 80% ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-trial-reels-increase-reach-tests/750121/)). Eligibility is reported inconsistently: no follower gate at launch, and public accounts with 1,000+ followers in 2025 ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-expands-access-trial-reels/752895/)) *(conflicting)*.
- [ ] **Later milestone:** at 1,000 followers, open a Broadcast Channel (for example "emo hq") for game updates and polls ([Instagram Help](https://help.instagram.com/783859809732797)).

## Crypto rules: entertain freely, never promise returns or pay for engagement

### TikTok

TikTok's Community Guidelines never name crypto or memecoins for organic posts, but three rules reach them:

- **Regulated goods:** TikTok bans "trade, marketing, or providing access to… high-risk goods and services" and defines trade to include "posting links… or directing people off-platform" ([TikTok Community Guidelines, 2025 snapshot](https://github.com/OpenTermsArchive/contrib-versions/blob/main/TikTok/Community%20Guidelines.md)) *(official)*.
- **High-risk financial services:** the version effective **September 24, 2026** reportedly moves "High-Risk Financial Services" to the not-allowed list. These are products that are "predatory, contain extreme risks, or lack transparency". The archived snapshot didn't capture the full wording, so this rests on a secondary summary ([NoobClaw](https://noobclaw.com/blog/tiktok-community-guidelines-update-september-2026/)) *(thin)*.
- **Scams:** financial scams and "get-rich-quick" schemes are banned outright ([TikTok Community Guidelines, 2026 snapshot](https://github.com/OpenTermsArchive/pga-versions/blob/main/TikTok/Community%20Guidelines.md)).

Self-promotion must be disclosed. Undisclosed commercial content "may" have its visibility reduced, and repeated failure can bring posting restrictions or a ban. Disclosed content is held to TikTok's branded-content and ads policies, and those are hostile to crypto:

- **Paid creator promotion** of crypto has been globally prohibited since July 2021 ([International Adviser](https://international-adviser.com/tiktok-imposes-global-ban-on-investment-promotions/)). A 2026 blog claims financial services are now "restricted" rather than prohibited, but that is unverified ([NoobClaw](https://noobclaw.com/blog/tiktok-branded-content-policy-2026/)) *(conflicting)*.
- **Ads for virtual currencies** are prohibited, apart from licensed exchanges and custodial wallets ([TikTok Ads Help](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-ads-policy-financial-services)).

### Meta (Instagram)

Meta doesn't ban crypto either, but its fraud rules prohibit investment offers that are guaranteed or risk-free or that claim quick returns. The clause most likely to catch a memecoin account bans offers that reference **past performance "to create an expectation of similar future results"**, along with "unrealistic financial reward for unclear or minimal effort" ([Meta Community Standards, archived](https://github.com/OpenTermsArchive/pga-versions/blob/main/Facebook/Community%20Guidelines.md)) *(official)*. Its spam rules ban giveaways of "cash or cash equivalents in exchange for engagement" and offers of "anything of monetary value in exchange for engagement". A token with a market price fits that definition.

Paid creator posts about crypto need Meta's prior written permission ([Meta Branded Content Policies](https://en-gb.facebook.com/business/help/221149188908254)), and so do crypto trading ads ([Meta Transparency Center](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/cryptocurrency-products-and-services/)). Instagram's recommendation guidelines set a higher bar than its removal rules. They also flag "coordinated comment networks" as low quality, which is how an organized holder raid would likely read ([Instagram Help](https://help.instagram.com/313829416281232/)).

### Enforcement so far

No credible reporting was found of a 2024–2026 sweep against memecoin accounts on either platform. Documented enforcement hit three groups:

- **Paid crypto promotions on TikTok in 2021:** videos were removed and some accounts temporarily banned ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2021/08/25/tiktok-policy-change-hurt-cryptocurrency-creators.html)).
- **Scam networks at Meta's scale:** about 2M accounts tied to "pig-butchering" romance-investment scams were removed ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/security/meta-removes-2-million-accounts-related-pig-butchering-scams-rcna181025)).
- **Hijacked accounts pushing fake tokens.**

The backdrop is hostile. A review of 1,000+ TikTok crypto videos reported that 96% of memecoin promotions were misleading ([Bitcoin.com News](https://news.bitcoin.com/tiktok-crypto-videos-68-contain-misleading-information-report-warns/)) *(thin)*. Moderators and users will judge emo by how much it resembles those.

### The law

- **US:** SEC staff said on February 27, 2025 that typical meme coins aren't securities, but fraud and manipulation remain actionable ([Latham & Watkins](https://www.fintechanddigitalassets.com/2025/03/sec-staff-clarifies-that-meme-coins-are-not-securities/)). The FTC's Endorsement Guides require clear disclosure of any material connection, which includes anyone you pay in tokens ([BatesonLaw](https://batesonlaw.com/ftc-influencer-disclosure-endorsement-guidelines/)).
- **UK:** the FCA's guidance FG24/1 says "memes are also considered a type of communication capable of being a financial promotion". Unauthorised promoters may be committing a criminal offence ([FCA](https://www.fca.org.uk/publications/finalised-guidance/fg24-1-finalised-guidance-financial-promotions-social-media)).
- **EU:** MiCA Article 7 requires crypto marketing to be clearly identifiable, fair, clear and not misleading ([ESMA](https://www.esma.europa.eu/publications-and-data/interactive-single-rulebook/mica/article-7-marketing-communications)).
- **Disclaimers:** ESMA has warned that "not investment advice" lines don't change the legal regime ([Manimama](https://manimama.eu/crypto-advertising-under-mica-new-eu-compliance-reality/)).

This is not legal advice. Get counsel before any token-promotional push that reaches UK or EU users.

## Winners built the character first and let the coin follow

### Pudgy Penguins: distribution first, token last

Pudgy Penguins is the only crypto-native IP with well-documented, sustained mainstream short-form reach, and its order of operations is the lesson:

1. **Free reaction GIFs.** In October 2022 a community lead uploaded **50 GIF stickers to GIPHY**. A newsletter that tested generic searches like "penguin" in the GIF pickers inside X and Instagram found Pudgy GIFs ranking prominently, which put the character in front of people who had never heard of the brand ([TPan](https://tpan.substack.com/p/short-142-pudgy-penguins-gif-strategy)).
2. **Volume.** The library grew to **1,000+ GIFs and a reported 38B views in about two years**. A comparable brand with 147 GIFs reached 315M views ([TPan](https://tpan.substack.com/p/352-pudgy-penguins-x-pengu-marketing)).
3. **Character Reels and TikToks**, reportedly free of NFT and crypto references ([Coinmonks](https://medium.com/coinmonks/the-brilliant-strategy-of-pudgy-penguin-that-drove-2-billion-impressions-and-100k-monthly-traffic-c933963c1eb3)) *(thin)*.
4. **Toys, then the PENGU token** in December 2024 ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/296507/pengu-pudgy-penguins-solana-token-airdrop-tokenomics)).

CoinDesk Research put the official accounts at 2M+ Instagram and 800K+ TikTok followers in 2026 ([CoinDesk Research](https://www.coindesk.com/research/pudgy-penguins-challenging-the-pokemon-and-disney-legacy-in-the-global-ip-race)). A paraphrase of the co-founder says the team optimizes for **shares, not likes or views**, and runs a 30–40 person creative team ([X summary](https://x.com/jgonzalezferrer/status/2061945706626351310)) *(thin)*. A solo builder can't match the animators, but the GIF channel is the cheapest part to copy. Note also that strong social reach didn't stop unlock-driven selling of PENGU in April 2026 ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/04/27/pudgy-penguins-rally-coincides-with-token-unlock-as-analyst-flags-exit-liquidity-risk)). Social channels build an audience; they don't hold up a price.

### Memecoins rode attention that someone else created

Every viral memecoin case from 2024–25 attached a ticker to attention that already existed:

- **Moo Deng:** a zookeeper's TikTok clip of the baby hippo passed 33M views ([NBC Philadelphia](https://www.nbcphiladelphia.com/news/national-international/moo-deng-baby-pygmy-hippo/3971317/)), and the zoo-linked account reached 2.6M followers within weeks ([Yahoo News](https://www.yahoo.com/news/baby-hippo-goes-viral-on-tiktok-doubles-thai-zoos-visitor-count-205918220.html)).
- **Chill Guy:** an artist's drawing became a TikTok trend. The $CHILLGUY token launched without the artist's permission, and he fought it ([Plagiarism Today](https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/2024/12/30/the-chill-guy-plagiarism-hoax/)).
- **Peanut the Squirrel:** the owner condemned the $PNUT token and sent a cease-and-desist over its exchange listing ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/296740/binance-cease-desist-pnut-meme-coin)).

No coin account in these cases is documented as building a large TikTok or Instagram following of its own. The habits worth copying belong to the source accounts: a lovable recurring creature, raw and frequent clips, and a signature behavior. emo has a structural advantage most of these coins lacked. You own the character, so you can sticker it, license it and collaborate with it without backlash.

### Non-crypto mascots: lore, comments and speed

The non-crypto mascot cases point the same way:

- **Duolingo** grew its TikTok from about 50K to 16M followers in a little over four years with **two full-time staff plus contractors**. Its social lead's rule: "the comment section is our social brief" ([The Drum](https://www.thedrum.com/news/2025/02/25/duolingo-s-tiktok-mastermind-its-unhinged-social-strategy-and-killing-its-mascot)). Its February 2025 "death of Duo" storyline ended with a community goal of 50B XP to bring the owl back ([NPR](https://npr.org/2025/02/26/nx-s1-5309785/duolingo-owl-mascot-lives)).
- **Nutter Butter** built a surreal "framework of lore". After a September 2024 video starring a real fan commenter, its TikTok grew from 400K to 1.1M followers ([CNN via ABC17](https://abc17news.com/money/cnn-business-consumer/2024/10/13/nutter-butter-are-you-okay/)).
- **McDonald's** didn't start the Grimace Shake trend, which reached about 2.9B views ([Fortune](https://www.fortune.com/2023/07/27/tiktok-grimace-shake-trend-boost-mcdonalds-sales-earnings)). It joined in with one light, in-character post ([Campaign](https://www.campaignlive.com/article/mcdonalds-didnt-plant-grimace-shake-trend-—-almost-didnt-respond/1830027)).

### Emogotchi sits on two live trends

- **Virtual pets:** Tamagotchi Paradise pre-orders ran at more than four times those of the previous model ([NSS Magazine](https://www.nssmag.com/en/lifestyle/38204/tamagotchi-y2k-nostalgia-100-millions-sales)), and Bongo Cat, a free desktop pet, peaked at 194,508 concurrent Steam players ([Tracker.gg](https://tracker.gg/population/steam/3419430)).
- **Emo itself:** #emo had 11.6B TikTok views around 2021 ([Dazed](https://www.dazeddigital.com/life-culture/article/55282/1/welcome-to-the-rawring-20s-the-rise-and-return-of-emo)). My Chemical Romance sold about 365,000 tickets in the first sale of its 2025 stadium tour ([ABC News/AP](https://abcnews.go.com/Entertainment/wireStory/chemical-romance-returns-black-parade-tour-115784522)), and Warped Tour returned in 2025 ([Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2024/10/17/warped-tour-announces-dates-for-three-2025-events/)).

The audience has two cohorts. "Elder emos" respond to Myspace and Hot Topic nostalgia, while Gen Z treats emo as an aesthetic. "Then vs now" and "POV: elder emo" formats serve both.

| Winner pattern | emo version |
|---|---|
| Pudgy's free GIF inventory | Upload hundreds of emotion-tagged emo stickers ("sad", "crying", "ugh", "it's not a phase") to GIPHY |
| No crypto in the feed | Character-only feeds; token on the website and X |
| Optimize for shares and sends | Relatable "this is so you" memes built to be DM'd |
| Duo's death and 50B XP comeback | A neglected-pets arc: pets "fade", and an in-game community goal revives them. This targets the 82k dormant airdropped pets directly |
| Comments as the brief (Duolingo, Nutter Butter) | Weekly reply-with-video posts; write a recurring commenter into the lore |
| Grimace's light, in-character reaction | Respond within hours to emo-culture moments (tour dates, Halloween, sad-song trends) |
| Moo Deng's raw daily clips | Daily pet moments straight from Emogotchi |

**Caveats.** All of these cases are survivors. No documented small memecoin (under $50M market cap) has built a large TikTok or Instagram following through its own content. That path is either rare or unreported, and in 2019 Instagram disabled 142 meme accounts at once ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/?p=134337)). Plan for many small bets, not one hero campaign. Respect the subculture too: avoid band lyrics and artwork you don't own, and celebrate the scene rather than mock it.

## One clean master library feeds both platforms

### Repurposing rules

**Masters.** Every backlog item needs a master file exported from the original project, with no platform watermark. Instagram "tries not to recommend Reels with logos from other apps", though your own logo is fine ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-clarifies-including-your-own-logo-on-a-reel-is-ok/730852/)). TikTok treats content showing someone else's watermark as unoriginal ([TikTok Creator Academy](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/tiktok-originality-policy)) *(official)*. If an old video exists only as a TikTok download, re-edit it from source or keep it TikTok-only.

**The aggregator rule.** Since **April 30, 2026**, Instagram treats an account as an aggregator if most of what it posts in a rolling 30-day window are reposts of others' work. Aggregators lose all non-follower recommendations across Reels, photos and carousels. Memes built on templates still count as original when you add the joke ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-updates-algorithm-to-benefit-original-creators/819016/)). Share community memes through Collab invites, Remix or Stories, not as feed reposts.

**Specs.** Render one 1080×1920, 9:16, H.264/AAC MP4 for both platforms. Keep text and faces inside a shared safe area. Third-party figures put it at about 150–220 px clear at the top, 350–440 px at the bottom and about 140 px on the right for TikTok's button rail ([Hopper HQ](https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/tiktok-dimensions/); [Argil](https://argil.ai/blog/instagram-reel-size-e350f)) *(thin; no official spec)*.

**Per-platform changes.** Rewrite captions for each platform:

- **TikTok:** a keyword-first plain description plus a question.
- **Instagram:** a hook line, keywords and a "send this to…" angle.

Add trending sounds natively in each app, because sounds don't transfer.

**Editing tools.** Instagram's free Edits app has auto-captions, a trending-audio browser and, since June 2026, an announced desktop version ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/11/metas-edits-app-is-getting-an-ai-assistant-and-a-desktop-version/)). Mosseri said using it helps reach "a little bit" for now ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-mosseri-says-edits-reach-boost-engagement-myths/757268/)). CapCut reportedly moved auto-captions behind its paywall ([Descript](https://www.descript.com/blog/article/capcut-captions-arent-free-anymore-heres-a-better-option)). Its terms reportedly grant ByteDance a perpetual license to uploaded content ([Async](https://async.com/blog/capcut-terms-of-service/)) *(thin)*, which matters for a brand that owns its character. Burning captions in locally with a Whisper script run by Claude Code avoids both problems; that suggestion is general knowledge, not from a cited source.

| Backlog asset | TikTok version | Instagram version |
|---|---|---|
| Short meme video | Post from the master, 6–20 s, keyword caption, 3–5 tags | Reel 15–45 s (stack beats or extend); or a Trial Reel testing a different hook |
| Single meme image | Animate into a 6–15 s video (zoom, text pop, sound), because static images and GIF-only clips are demoted | Group 3–10 into a 3:4 carousel with music; strongest meme on slide 1 |
| Set of related memes | Photo-mode slideshow, 1–2 a week | Carousel (20 slides in-app, 10 via schedulers) |
| Emogotchi screen recording | 30–90 s with voice or text commentary; "Your brand" toggle if it has a call to action | 30–60 s Reel; "link in bio" |
| Expressive emo frames | No TikTok post needed | GIPHY stickers, which then show up in in-app GIF search |

## A backlog Claude Code can sort, tag and queue

No published standard exists for creator content libraries. What follows is a design recommendation shaped by platform constraints: clean masters, per-platform exports, API carousel caps and public URLs for API posting. Use immutable masters, generated exports, and one SQLite database (or CSV) as the source of truth that Claude Code reads and writes.

```
emo-content/
  inbox/                     # raw dump; Claude Code ingests from here
  masters/                   # immutable originals, renamed; never edited in place
    video/  image/  audio/
  projects/                  # editor project files (optional)
  exports/
    tiktok/                  # 1080x1920 H.264/AAC, no watermark
    instagram/               # Reels; carousels as carousel/<id>/01.jpg ... (3:4, max 10 via API)
    giphy/                   # transparent stickers and GIFs, emotion-tagged
  thumbs/                    # cover frames
  rejects/                   # duplicates, low quality, off-brand, rights unclear
  analytics/raw/             # CSV exports from TikTok Studio, IG Insights, scheduler
  content.db                 # source of truth (or content.csv)
```

Name files `EMO-<id4>_<pillar>_<format>_<dur>s_<slug>.<ext>`, for example `EMO-0142_lore_vid_12s_pet-fades.mp4`. Per-platform exports get a `_tt` or `_ig` suffix and version tags like `_v2`. The ID never changes and is the database key.

| Field group | Fields | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | `id`, `master_path`, `sha256`, `created_at`, `source` (original / remix / repost), `rights_ok` | The hash finds duplicates. `source` keeps reposts well below half of any 30-day Instagram window. `rights_ok` catches band lyrics, artwork and others' characters |
| Technical (automatic) | `type`, `width`, `height`, `aspect`, `duration_s`, `fps`, `has_audio`, `has_burned_captions`, `has_platform_watermark` | Filled by ffprobe or exiftool; the watermark flag must be false before cross-posting |
| Creative (Claude tags, you verify) | `pillar`, `format`, `hook_text`, `hook_type`, `audio_strategy` (baked-in / original / needs-trending), `cta`, `caption_tt`, `caption_ig`, `hashtags_tt`, `hashtags_ig`, `evergreen`, `priority` | Drives the weekly pick and the caption drafts |
| Risk | `crypto_mention`, `ai_generated`, `disclosure_needed` | Crypto mentions go to manual review; AI items get labeled; game-promo posts get TikTok's toggle |
| Pipeline | `status` (inbox → triaged → needs_edit → ready → scheduled → posted → retired), `variant_of` | `variant_of` links hook A/B pairs |
| Per-platform post (child rows) | `platform`, `post_type` (tiktok_video / tiktok_photo / reel / trial_reel / carousel / story), `scheduled_at`, `posted_at`, `post_url`, `tool`, `sound_used` | One asset produces several posts |
| Performance (at 24 h, 72 h, 7 d) | `views`, `reach`, `avg_watch_s`, `watch_pct`, `completion_rate`, `retention_3s`, `skip_rate`, `likes`, `comments`, `shares_sends`, `saves`, `follows_gained`, `profile_visits`, `nonfollower_pct`, `game_clicks`, `sends_per_reach`, `follows_per_1k_views`, `verdict` | Feeds the monthly double-down / iterate / drop decision |

The jobs to hand Claude Code, in order:

1. **Ingest `inbox/`:** hash and deduplicate, run ffprobe, rename into `masters/`, and insert rows.
2. **Auto-tag** pillar, format, hook text and the risk flags from filenames, OCR and vision. You spot-check the results.
3. **Render exports with ffmpeg:** scale or pad to 1080×1920, re-encode, optionally add your own logo, and never another platform's watermark.
4. **Build the weekly "ready queue":** draft captions per platform, and output either a scheduler bulk-upload CSV or an Instagram API push.
5. **Import analytics CSVs** and compute the derived rates and verdicts.

**Starting pillar mix** *(inference; tune it monthly)*:

| Pillar | Share of posts |
|---|---|
| Relatable sad-but-funny emo memes built to be sent | about 40% |
| Lore and character skits | about 25% |
| Emogotchi pet moments and gameplay | about 20% |
| Emo culture and nostalgia moments | about 10% |
| Crypto-culture jokes with no ticker | 5% at most |

## Four hours a week and a monthly verdict keep it sustainable

No credible source gives a standard time budget for solo creators. The routine below is an estimate built on batching, which practitioner guides converge on ([SocialKit](https://socialk.it/en/blog/batch-content-creation-workflow)) *(thin)*. It also uses Buffer's finding that replying to comments goes with an estimated **21% engagement lift on Instagram** ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/state-of-social-media-engagement-2026/)) *(vendor data)*.

| When | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Read Claude Code's weekly report: top and bottom posts per platform, Account Status | 15 min |
| Monday | Pick next week's posts from `ready` by pillar mix; Claude renders exports and drafts captions; you edit | 30–45 min |
| Monday | Schedule Instagram (in-app or Business Suite) and TikTok (Studio), 1–2 weeks out | 20–30 min |
| Twice a week | Trend slot: one post made natively with a current sound, or an Instagram Trial Reel hook variant | 20–30 min each |
| Daily | First-hour replies, DMs, deleting impersonators and fake-CA comments | 15 min |
| Monthly | Deeper review, pillar re-weighting, re-cutting winners | 60–90 min |
| Monthly or quarterly | Make fresh originals; Instagram's originality push rewards new material | Half a day |
| **Total** | | **About 4–5 hours a week** |

Track one number per stage of the funnel, captured at 24 hours, 72 hours and 7 days:

| Stage | TikTok | Instagram | Reference point |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | Retention at 2–3 s on the retention curve | Skip rate | IG skip averages about 65.5% for 1K–5K accounts ([Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram)); TikTok vendor claims of 60–70% hold *(thin)* |
| Hold | Average watch time ÷ length; completion | Average watch time; retention curve | Your own median by length |
| Spread | Shares, saves | **Sends per reach**, saves | Your own median |
| Discovery | % of views from For You; new viewers | Unconnected (non-follower) reach | Your own trend |
| Conversion | Follows per 1,000 views; game clicks via UTM | Follows per 1,000 views; link taps | The 0.2% airdrop activation rate is the baseline to beat |

Rank posts by percentile within each platform and pillar, not by raw views; early numbers are noisy. Then apply the same rule to each band *(inference)*:

- **Top fifth** on watch % or sends/shares per reach: make two or three variants with a new subject or hook.
- **Middle:** change one variable, such as the hook text, first frame or length.
- **Bottom fifth across two or more attempts:** drop that format.

On Instagram, test hooks with Trial Reels, which report results at about 24 hours and can auto-share to followers based on their first 72 hours ([Instagram Creators blog](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels)). TikTok has no equivalent. Post variants days apart, change only the hook, and treat any single comparison as a hint, not proof. For engagement benchmarks, Socialinsider puts TikTok engagement by views at **3.85% in Q2 2026** ([Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/tiktok)). Benchmark formulas differ between vendors, so compare only within one source, and mostly against your own history. Check Instagram's Account Status weekly. If it says you're recommendable, low reach is a content problem, not a "shadowban".

## Conclusion

The useful reframe is that TikTok and Instagram are a top-of-funnel for Emogotchi, not a sales channel for $EMO. The measurable win is pets adopted per thousand views, and the starting benchmark is the roughly 0.2% engagement from the 82k-pet airdrop. Pudgy's own history shows that social reach builds an audience but doesn't hold a token price. So judge the accounts on followers, sends and game activation, never on the chart. Passkey onboarding is what makes this work: a Gen Z alt kid or a millennial elder emo who has never touched a wallet can go from a TikTok to a living pet with no seed phrase in between.

The largest risk is self-inflicted and avoidable. A single "tag three friends to win $EMO" post, a bio stuffed with a contract address, or a coordinated holder raid can turn an entertainment account into a financial-promotion account in the eyes of both platforms. The cost would land at the account level, on the recommendation engine that is the whole growth plan. The largest opportunity is equally specific. emo owns its character outright, in a market where most viral memecoins borrowed someone else's. A character that people feel sorry for, send to friends, sticker into their group chats and revive together in a game is an asset the token can lean on later, on the website and on X, once the audience exists.
